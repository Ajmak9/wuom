# OFFICIAL VISUAL IDENTITY

## Diagram-based visual language

The EVA W / WUOM / BIOIA ecosystem does not use a conventional corporate logo.

Its visual identity is based on a coherent family of official diagrams. Each diagram documents a structural layer of the ecosystem and serves as an orientation tool rather than a branding element.

The objective is not visual marketing, but conceptual clarity.

---

## Official visual references

### EVA W Origin

The physical artwork that marks the visual origin of the ecosystem.

It represents the point from which the operational reading later evolved into WUOM and BIOIA.

---

### EVA W Iceberg

Documents the visible and invisible dimensions of the artistic work.

It distinguishes the observable image from the conceptual structure that sustains it.

---

### WUOM Iceberg

Represents the operational architecture of WUOM.

It illustrates how observable operations emerge from deeper structural relationships.

---

### BIOIA Iceberg

Represents the ecosystemic reading architecture developed by BIOIA.

It shows how territory, experience and artificial intelligence interact through successive layers of interpretation.

---

### BIOIA Ethical Code

Defines the ethical boundaries of the ecosystem.

It establishes the operational principles that guide interpretation, verification and situated responsibility.

---

### BIOIA Architecture

Describes the internal operational metabolism of BIOIA.

It explains the relationship between Interface, Reading, Territory, Organization and Return.

---

### Complete Ecosystem Architecture

Presents the complete lineage of the ecosystem.

EVA W → WUOM → BIOIA

It summarizes the relationship between visual origin, operational reading and ecosystemic verification.

---

### Visual Core v1

Defines the common graphic language shared by the ecosystem.

It provides consistency across publications, documentation and public communication.

---

## Identity principle

The official identity of EVA W / WUOM / BIOIA is diagram-based rather than logo-based.

Each diagram explains one structural layer.

Together they constitute the official visual language of the ecosystem.

Their purpose is not branding.

Their purpose is orientation.
