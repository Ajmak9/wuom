What most people encounter.

---

### BELOW THE WATER

What sustains the work.

* Long-Term Practice
* Observation
* Territory
* Visual Memory
* Experimentation
* Continuity
* Care
* Time

---

### Why an iceberg?

Most of EVA W is not hidden by intention.

It simply exists below the level of immediate visibility.

The visible works are the result of years of observation, experimentation, continuity and care.

The artwork can be seen immediately.

The conditions that made it possible usually cannot.

---

### Working Principle

The visible image is not the origin.

It is the result.

What remains unseen is often what sustains everything else.
