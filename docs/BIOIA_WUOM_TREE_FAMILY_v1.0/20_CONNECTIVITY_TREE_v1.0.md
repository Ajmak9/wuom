# 20. CONNECTIVITY TREE v1.0
## BIOIA / WUOM

### Reading connections to understand and sustain living territories

The **Connectivity Tree** organizes territorial connectivity as the network that allows ecosystems, people, water, knowledge and information to remain connected over time.

Connectivity is more than physical infrastructure. It is the capacity of a territory to maintain ecological, social and informational relationships that sustain resilience and adaptive management.

Within BIOIA / WUOM, connectivity is understood as a living property of healthy territories.

> **Without connectivity there is fragmentation. Without return there is disconnection.**

---

## Reading dimensions

The Connectivity Tree integrates five complementary dimensions:

- **Territory:** ecological corridors, rivers, landscapes and human settlements.
- **Data:** interoperable, traceable and quality-controlled spatial information.
- **Experience:** field observation, local knowledge and interdisciplinary collaboration.
- **AI for reading:** pattern recognition, network analysis and identification of connectivity changes.
- **Return:** verified information that supports territorial coordination and adaptive management.

---

## Functions of the Connectivity Tree

- Identify ecological and territorial connections.
- Detect fragmentation and emerging disconnections.
- Support integrated landscape planning.
- Connect environmental, social and governance information.
- Improve territorial resilience through connected systems.
- Promote cooperation through verified territorial knowledge.

---

## What it is not

The Connectivity Tree is **not**:

- a surveillance network,
- a mass monitoring platform,
- data extraction without purpose,
- technology replacing local knowledge,
- artificial intelligence making territorial decisions.

Its purpose is to strengthen relationships that sustain living territories.

---

## BIOIA / WUOM orientation

Healthy territories depend on connected ecosystems, connected knowledge and connected communities.

Artificial intelligence helps reveal relationships.

People interpret them.

The territory verifies them.

---

## Key idea

**Connectivity is not only about linking places.  
It is about sustaining relationships.**
