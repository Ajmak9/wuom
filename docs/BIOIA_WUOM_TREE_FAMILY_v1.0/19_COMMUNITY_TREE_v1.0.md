# 19. COMMUNITY TREE v1.0
## BIOIA / WUOM

### Reading communities to strengthen living territories

The **Community Tree** recognizes that territories are shaped by people.

Communities preserve memory, local knowledge, stewardship practices and collective responsibility. They connect ecological understanding with daily life and transform information into coordinated action.

Within BIOIA / WUOM, communities are not external actors. They are an essential part of the living system.

> **Without people there is no stewardship. Without communities there is no continuity.**

---

## Reading dimensions

The Community Tree integrates five complementary dimensions:

- **Territory:** shared landscapes, cultural heritage, local resources and common futures.
- **Data:** transparent, accessible and traceable information that supports collective decisions.
- **Experience:** local knowledge, lived experience, traditional practices and scientific collaboration.
- **AI for reading:** artificial intelligence that connects information, identifies patterns and supports understanding without replacing human judgement.
- **Return:** knowledge that comes back to communities to improve resilience, participation and territorial care.

---

## Functions of the Community Tree

- Strengthen community participation and stewardship.
- Connect people, knowledge and local initiatives.
- Identify shared challenges and opportunities.
- Support collaborative environmental action.
- Improve social resilience and territorial governance.
- Encourage intergenerational continuity and shared responsibility.

---

## What it is not

The Community Tree is **not**:

- a top-down governance model,
- a surveillance platform,
- a substitute for local participation,
- artificial intelligence replacing community decisions,
- a system for extracting knowledge without return.

Its purpose is to strengthen cooperation through shared understanding.

---

## BIOIA / WUOM orientation

Healthy territories depend on healthy relationships between people, ecosystems and knowledge.

Artificial intelligence helps organize information.

Communities provide meaning.

The territory provides verification.

---

## Key idea

**Communities are not beneficiaries of the territory.  
They are co-creators of its future.**
