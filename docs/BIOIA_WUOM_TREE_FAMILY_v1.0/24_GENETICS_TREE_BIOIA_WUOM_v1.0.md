# 24 Genetics Tree v1.0

**BIOIA / WUOM**

*A perspective for reading the genetic heritage of life within living systems.*

---

## Overview

The Genetics Tree reads genetic diversity as one of the foundations of living systems.

It does not reduce genetics to DNA sequences, biotechnology or laboratory analysis.

It understands genetics as the living heritage that enables adaptation, resilience, evolution and continuity across generations.

Genetic diversity is not simply biological information.

It is one of the conditions that makes life possible.

---

## Purpose

The Genetics Tree helps to:

- Understand genetic diversity within living systems.
- Recognize the importance of genetic heritage.
- Relate genetics to biodiversity, adaptation and resilience.
- Support conservation of genetic resources.
- Promote responsible stewardship of living heritage.
- Guide decisions through territorial return.

Its function is not to manipulate genetics.

Its function is to understand the role genetic diversity plays in sustaining life.

---

## Central Question

> How does genetic diversity contribute to the continuity, resilience and adaptive capacity of this living system?
