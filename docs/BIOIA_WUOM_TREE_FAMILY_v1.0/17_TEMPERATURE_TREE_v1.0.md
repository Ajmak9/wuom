17. TEMPERATURE TREE v1.0
BIOIA / WUOM
Reading thermal signals to understand, adapt and protect the territory

The Temperature Tree organizes thermal information as a layer of territorial understanding.

Temperature is not merely a meteorological variable. It is an ecological signal that reflects how water, vegetation, soils, atmosphere and human activities interact over time.

Within BIOIA / WUOM, thermal data becomes meaningful only when interpreted together with territorial context and verified observations.

Temperature is a signal, not a verdict.

Reading dimensions

The Temperature Tree integrates five complementary dimensions:

Territory: topography, vegetation, soils, water and landscape structure.
Data: air, land and surface temperature from reliable and traceable sources.
Experience: field observation, local ecological knowledge and scientific expertise.
AI for reading: pattern recognition, anomaly detection and contextual interpretation.
Return: information that comes back to the territory to improve decisions and resilience.
Functions of the Temperature Tree
Monitor thermal dynamics across landscapes.
Detect heat trends and emerging anomalies.
Relate temperature with water availability, vegetation and ecosystem health.
Support adaptation to climate change.
Improve territorial resilience through verified observations.
Transform data into practical ecological guidance.
What it is not

The Temperature Tree is not:

a weather forecast,
an alarm platform,
a surveillance system,
a substitute for field observation,
artificial intelligence making decisions alone.

Its purpose is to improve territorial understanding through contextual reading.

BIOIA / WUOM orientation

Thermal information only becomes useful when it is connected with ecology, experience and territory.

Artificial intelligence helps detect patterns.

People provide interpretation.

The territory provides verification.

Key idea

Reading temperature means reading the condition of a living territory.
