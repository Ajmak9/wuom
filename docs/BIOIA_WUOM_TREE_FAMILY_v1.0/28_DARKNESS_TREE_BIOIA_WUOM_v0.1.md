# Purpose

To explore whether darkness constitutes a territorial dimension with sufficient entity to justify, in the future, its own interface within BIOIA/WUOM.

This document does not create a new interface.

It incorporates a new tree into the nursery for methodological evaluation.

---

# Initial Question

> **Does darkness express its own territorial organization, or can it be interpreted sufficiently through the existing interfaces?**

This question guides the entire exploration.

---

# Observed Territorial Signals

Darkness appears recurrently in relation to:

- light pollution;
- nocturnal rhythms;
- nocturnal fauna;
- rest and recovery;
- the night sky;
- orientation and navigation;
- observation of the territory;
- perception of the landscape;
- nocturnal culture and heritage;
- health and well-being.

These signals justify its incorporation into the nursery.

This hypothesis must be verified.
