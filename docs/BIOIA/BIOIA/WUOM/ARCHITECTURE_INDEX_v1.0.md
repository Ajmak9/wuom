# BIOIA / WUOM — Architecture Index v1.0

## Purpose

This section brings together the structural documents that define how BIOIA / WUOM is organized.

The Architecture layer explains **how the ecosystem operates**.

The Metaarchitecture layer explains **how the ecosystem governs its own evolution**.

Together, they provide a coherent framework for reading, orienting and developing BIOIA / WUOM without losing consistency.

---

## Architecture Structure

### 01. BIOIA / WUOM Operative Architecture

Describes the operational structure of BIOIA / WUOM.

It presents the main components of the ecosystem and the relationships between territory, observation, data, experience, artificial intelligence, return and the operational trees.

It answers:

> **How is BIOIA / WUOM organized?**

---

### 02. Ecosystemic Governance Metaarchitecture

Defines the governance model that protects the coherence and long-term evolution of BIOIA / WUOM.

Instead of adding new operational components, it establishes the principles that regulate when, why and how the architecture itself may evolve.

It answers:

> **How does BIOIA / WUOM govern its own evolution?**

---

## Relationship

| Document | Function |
|---|---|
| **01. Operative Architecture** | Organizes the ecosystem. |
| **02. Ecosystemic Governance Metaarchitecture** | Governs the evolution of that organization. |

Architecture builds the system.

Metaarchitecture preserves its coherence.

---

## Guiding Principle

BIOIA / WUOM does not seek unlimited expansion.

Every structural evolution should emerge from verified operational necessity, maintain coherence across the ecosystem, and strengthen the system’s capacity for situated reading and responsible orientation.
