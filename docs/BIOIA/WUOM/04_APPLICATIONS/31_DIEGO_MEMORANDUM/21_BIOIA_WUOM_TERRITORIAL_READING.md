# PROTECTION, COMPENSATION AND TERRITORIAL TIME

## The second tree appears in the file before it exists in the territory.

> **CENTRAL THESIS**
>
> Accounting immediately adds what the territory can only verify over time. Once the loss has occurred, planting one specimen and commissioning a study is not equivalent to having protected the trees that were removed.

## 1. The apparent operation

The operation appears simple: there were ten trees, nine are removed, one remains and another is planted. On paper, there are two trees.

## 10 🌳 − 9 🌳 + 1 🌳 = 2 🌳

### Administrative or accounting result

The problem is that the territory does not count units alone. It verifies functions, relationships, maturity and continuity.

## 2. Two non-equivalent trees

The tree that remains already has roots, a canopy, shade, associated soil, biodiversity, water-retention capacity and a history of adaptation to the place.

The added tree still has to become established, grow and survive. It requires water, resources, care and time. Meanwhile, it does not perform the same functions as the adult specimen that was removed.

| EXISTING TREE | NEW SPECIMEN | TERRITORIAL DIFFERENCE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Established roots | Uncertain rooting | Risk of mortality |
| Existing canopy and shade | Future canopy | Years without an equivalent function |
| Already active habitat | Potential habitat | Immediate loss of continuity |
| Associated water and soil | Requires water and maintenance | Additional cost |

## 10 🌳 − 9 🌳 + 1 🌱

## = 1 🌳 + 1 future possibility + 💰 + ⌛

### Material result during the replacement period

Throughout that time, the other eight trees remain absent, along with the shade, roots, habitat, retained water and territorial continuity they provided.

## 3. A study is added to protect the trees

A protection study is incorporated into the operation. It may analyse conservation, biodiversity, compensatory measures, monitoring of the new specimen and protection of the remaining tree.

## 10 🌳 − 9 🌳 + 1 🌱 + 🕵️📄

### The loss, the compensation and the study appear in the same file

But the study arrives after the loss:

## 🕵️ − 9 🌳

### Protection is studied after nine trees have already disappeared

Protection ceases to operate as prevention and instead begins to manage the consequences. The document may produce knowledge, recommendations and indicators, but it does not return the nine trees that were removed.

> **DECISIVE DISTINCTION**
>
> Studying protection is not equivalent to having protected. Protecting the remaining tree does not automatically compensate for the removal of the other nine either.

## 4. The territorial order and the inverted order

| TERRITORIAL ORDER | INVERTED ORDER |
| --- | --- |
| Prior study → protection → conservation of 10 trees | − 9 trees → subsequent study → management of the loss |
| Knowledge precedes the decision and prevents the loss. | The study arrives once the loss has already occurred. |

The study may be technically correct and still occupy the wrong position in the sequence.

When it arrives after the removal, it no longer protects what has disappeared: it documents, compensates for or manages a loss that has already occurred.

## 5. The complete territorial formula

## 10 🌳 − 9 🌳 + 1 🌱 + 🕵️📄

## 🟰 1 🌳 ➕ 1 promise ➕ 1 study ➕ 💰 ➕ ⌛ ➕ risk

### The administrative sum does not restore material continuity

The second tree exists administratively from the day it is planted. Territorially, it is still a future possibility.

The study also consumes resources and time. While it is being drafted, assessed and implemented, the territory continues to function with nine fewer trees.

## 6. BIOIA / WUOM reading

Accounting counts units. The territory verifies functions, relationships, maturity, continuity and return.

## + 1 🌱 does not cancel − 9 🌳

## + ⌛ does not automatically repair the loss

The question is not merely how many trees are recorded at the end of the procedure. The question is what existed before, what has been removed, which functions have been lost and how long the territory will have to pay before the new planting can perform some of them.

> **CLOSING**
>
> Protection begins by preventing loss while what is meant to be protected still exists. After removal, the subject of the study is no longer protection alone: it is how to manage a loss that has already occurred.
