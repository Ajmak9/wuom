## 1. Classification: yes, but with a qualifier

> **Precise definition**
>
> This piece can be classified as a **BIOIA / WUOM operational meta-architecture of intervention**. It does not represent the ecosystem's entire meta-architecture, but the rule of order that every application, case, interface or institutional relationship must preserve.

It is a meta-architecture because it does not merely describe components or a sequence of tasks. It defines the conditions under which other architectures can organise themselves, intervene and be verified without inverting the territorial order.

Its function is to preserve a common grammar at different scales: an estate, a biodiversity case, a public policy, a WUOM interface, an institutional call or a governance system.

### 1.1 Architecture, methodology and meta-architecture

| LEVEL | MAIN QUESTION | FUNCTION WITHIN BIOIA / WUOM |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Architecture | What elements exist and how do they relate? | Describes territory, experience, AI, network, institutions, signals, layers and return. |
| Methodology | What operations are performed? | Organises reading, deliberation, situated action, adjustment and evaluation. |
| Operational meta-architecture | What order must every application preserve? | Establishes that 0 sustains, 1 makes legible, 2 integrates and orients, and 3 returns and verifies. |

> **Relationship with the corpus**
>
> The piece acts as a bridge between the BIOIA / WUOM Architecture and the Meta-architecture of Ecosystemic Governance. It does not replace those documents: it translates their order into a verifiable operational grammar.

## 2. The 0 · 1 · 2 · 3 formula

# 0 · TERRITORY + 1 · WUOM + 2 · BIOIA = 3 · RETURN

The numbering does not express a hierarchy of value or four equivalent modules. It expresses an ontological and operational relationship: each position fulfils a different, non-interchangeable function.

| POSITION | NATURE | FUNCTION | MUST NOT BE CONFUSED WITH | VERIFICATION QUESTION |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **0 · Territory** | Living, situated and material condition | Sustains, limits, records memory and verifies consequences | An input, resource or passive setting | What can this place sustain and recover? |
| **1 · WUOM** | Instrumental reading architecture | Makes signals, layers, relationships, pressures and gaps legible | A decision-maker or substitute for situated knowledge | What is happening and how is it related? |
| **2 · BIOIA** | Complete relational ecosystem | Integrates territory, experience, AI, network and institutions; recognises order or imbalance and provides orientation | A tool, a module or an autonomous AI | What order organises the relationship and whom does it serve? |
| **3 · Return** | Test of integrity and continuity | Returns capacities, knowledge, resources, care and a margin for recovery | A final outcome or ornamental compensation | What does the territory receive, and how can it verify it? |

> **Functional synthesis**
>
> 0 sustains. 1 makes legible. 2 integrates and orients. 3 returns and verifies.

## 3. Distinctions that protect the order

### 3.1 Territory is not the first step: it is the ground beneath every step

Position 0 does not mean that the territory appears at the beginning and then disappears. It remains beneath the entire sequence as condition, limit, memory, recovery capacity and verification base. No technical layer can replace that continuity.

### 3.2 WUOM makes legible; it does not decide

WUOM organises layers, lenses, maps, signals and relationships. It makes it possible to move from fragmented data to a situated reading. Its value lies in revealing configurations, not in imposing a purpose or automating the decision.

### 3.3 BIOIA is not a tool

BIOIA is the relational ecosystem in which territory, life, human experience, AI, network, institutions, pressures and consequences form a single field. It makes it possible to recognise whether that field preserves continuity or is organised in an inverted form.

For this reason, "BIOIA orients" is correct as a function, but insufficient as a definition. BIOIA also contains what must be read, the relationships that produce imbalance and the conditions from which reorientation can take place.

### 3.4 Return does not close the cycle: it keeps it open

Return is continuous. It must appear during the intervention through adjustment, access to knowledge, the return of capacities, reduction of pressures and restoration of the territorial margin. At the end, it verifies whether the relationship was reciprocal or extractive.

> **Ontological hierarchy**
>
> **Territory:** condition.  
> **WUOM:** reading instrument.  
> **BIOIA:** relational ecosystem.  
> **Return:** verification of integrity.

## 4. Operational grammar of intervention

The meta-architecture does not prescribe an identical formula for every place. It establishes an order that must be maintained while operations change according to the territory.

### A. Signal

Something occurs: a thermal variation, loss of water, infrastructure pressure, social displacement, ecological change or institutional conflict.

### B. Situated reading

The signal is contrasted with local experience, memory, temporality, limits and territorial relationships.

### C. WUOM legibility

Layers and lenses organise complexity without eliminating contradictions or isolating variables.

### D. BIOIA understanding

The whole makes it possible to recognise order, inversion, accumulation of pressure, actors, responsibilities and possibilities for return.

### E. Human and institutional deliberation

People and communities retain judgement. AI helps to read; it does not determine the purpose.

### F. Situated action

Intervention takes place with a scope, scale and temporality compatible with the place's recovery capacity.

### G. Return and verification

The territory receives capacities and can verify results, limits, unforeseen effects and the need for adjustment or cessation.

> **Decision rule**
>
> Orientation precedes action. When the reading is insufficient, the meta-architecture does not compel intervention: it may indicate a pause, reduction of scale or non-intervention.

## 5. BIOIA as a reading of order and imbalance

The meta-architecture makes visible that the problem does not lie solely in an isolated technology, institution or project. It lies in the order that assigns purpose, decision-making capacity, costs and return.

| DIMENSION | INVERTED ORDER | COEXISTENCE IN CONTINUITY |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose | The network defines the purpose. | The territory establishes conditions and limits; people deliberate upon the purpose. |
| AI | Optimises an already imposed direction. | Helps to read patterns, alternatives and consequences. |
| WUOM | Can be reduced to extractive instrumentation. | Makes signals and relationships legible without replacing situated knowledge. |
| Institutions | Manage growth and react to impacts. | Assume responsibility, custody and the capacity to stop or adjust. |
| Territory | Absorbs costs and appears at the end. | Remains the base, limit and verification. |
| Return | Late or non-existent compensation. | A compulsory, concrete and verifiable condition. |

> **Anchor formulation**
>
> WUOM leads towards a BIOIA reading. BIOIA contains the relational field that must be understood and reoriented. Return verifies that the intervention did not reproduce extraction.

## 6. Limits, pause and non-intervention

An ecosystemic meta-architecture must include the capacity not to act. The objective is not to maximise interventions, but to preserve the integrity of the relationship and prevent the tool from accelerating what it intended to understand.

### 6.1 Conditions for stopping or review

- There is insufficient situated information to understand the main relationships.
- The intervention exceeds the territory's ecological, social or cultural recovery capacity.
- There is no responsible actor for custody, monitoring and response to unforeseen effects.
- Return has not been defined, is merely declarative or cannot be verified from the place.
- Data gathering shifts towards surveillance, extraction, profiling or the exposure of sensitive information.
- Uncertainty and irreversibility advise increasing the safety margin, reducing scale or stopping.
- The network or institution demands that BIOIA adapt to an external purpose that inverts its order.

> **Precautionary criterion**
>
> Not knowing the exact limit does not authorise moving closer to it. It requires increasing the safety margin.

### 6.2 Recovery capacity

Every action must compare the time available with the territory's recovery time. When the former is shorter than the latter, continued pressure produces a progressive loss of equilibrium. The meta-architecture must detect this mismatch before the response is reduced to emergency containment.

## 7. Minimum verification matrix

The following matrix makes it possible to review an application before, during and after the intervention. It does not produce automatic certification: it organises custody questions.

| CRITERION | GREEN · CONTINUITY | AMBER · REVIEW | RED · STOP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Territory first | Conditions and limits defined from the place | Territory consulted late or partially | Territory appears only as a resource or support |
| WUOM reading | Layers and relationships verified with situated experience | Incomplete or disconnected data | Instrumentation used to justify a prior decision |
| BIOIA field | Order, pressures, actors and consequences integrated | Critical relationships unresolved | BIOIA reduced to a tool or brand |
| Decision | People and institutions deliberate and assume responsibility | Diffuse responsibility | AI or the network determines the purpose |
| Action | Scale and temporality compatible with recovery | Uncertain recovery margin | Risk of irreversible or cumulative harm |
| Return | Concrete, continuous and verifiable by the territory | Generic or late return | No return; only extraction or compensation |

### 7.1 Final question

> **Integrity test**
>
> After the intervention, does the territory have greater capacity to sustain life, read its conditions, deliberate and recover equilibrium, or has it merely surrendered data, resources and margin to the system?

## 8. Relationship with cases, interfaces and institutions

The operational meta-architecture can be applied to cases involving biodiversity, water, energy, heat, agriculture, mobility, governance, public AI or institutional design. The formula remains; the layers and operations change.

### 8.1 Cases and interfaces

- Real cases validate the grammar: they reveal specific pressures, actors, limits and returns.
- WUOM interfaces organise the reading, but do not replace BIOIA orientation or human deliberation.
- An interface can circulate independently once its architecture, language, utility and return have been consolidated.

### 8.2 Institutions and calls

- BIOIA does not adapt to a call in order to become eligible; the question is whether the call can relate to BIOIA without appropriating or distorting it.
- The institution must accept limits, custody, transparency, non-extraction and verifiable return.
- Participation does not transfer the BIOIA / WUOM core or turn the author into a brand, company or compulsory representative.

### 8.3 Custody and circulation

The conceptual core remains under custody. Derived tools and interfaces may circulate under conditions compatible with their integrity. Authorship identifies origin and responsibility; it does not turn the system into a personal showcase.
