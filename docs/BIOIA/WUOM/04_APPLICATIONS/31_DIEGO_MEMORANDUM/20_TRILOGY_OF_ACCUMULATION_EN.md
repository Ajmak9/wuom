## CENTRAL IDEA

Innovation does not constitute a problem in itself. Speed, artificial intelligence and time can increase a society's capacity to understand, coordinate and respond.

### What direction are they added to?

When the purpose remains unchanged, each new capacity amplifies the existing trajectory. It does not replace the previous system. It accumulates upon it.

## THE NEW DOES NOT REPLACE. IT IS ADDED.

**The question is not only what we incorporate, but what ceases to be done when the new appears.**

## I · MORE SPEED

Speed reduces distances and execution times. It makes it possible to produce, transport, build, communicate and extract more quickly.

But accelerating a trajectory does not change its direction. It only makes it possible to travel along it sooner.

When the purpose remains the same, speed also accelerates:

- extraction;
- pressure;
- costs;
- arrival at the limits.

## Speed does not correct the course. It intensifies it.

## II · MORE CAPACITY

Artificial intelligence adds reading, calculation, coordination and operational capacity. It can help us understand reality more clearly, optimise processes and detect patterns that are difficult to recognise in other ways.

But it does not determine the purpose by itself. If incorporated into an architecture that does not change direction, it amplifies that same architecture.

## ADDED CAPACITY + UNCHANGED PURPOSE

### = greater reach for the same trajectory

The new capacity does not necessarily replace the previous one. It is placed upon it and increases its reach.

## III · MORE TIME

Additional time can be used to deliberate, reduce pressure and change direction.

However, when the trajectory remains active, the time requested becomes a prolongation of the same process.

While the decision is pending:

- extraction continues;
- costs increase;
- the margin decreases.

## Each new ➕⌛ appears upon a different territorial base. Not the same one.

## THE COMMON PATTERN

The three capacities appear different. However, they produce the same effect when the purpose does not change.

| MORE SPEED | MORE CAPACITY | MORE TIME |
| --- | --- | --- |
| accelerates the trajectory | amplifies the trajectory | prolongs the trajectory |

**Meanwhile, the territorial base continues to bear the accumulation.**

## ACCUMULATION IS NOT REPLACEMENT

This also happens with energy. What is new does not always materially replace what came before. It is often added: new infrastructure, new sources, new networks and new capacities, without withdrawing the previous ones with the same intensity.

## TRANSITION ONLY REPLACES WHEN SOMETHING MATERIALLY DECREASES.

### If nothing decreases, total capacity accumulates.

The result is increasing accumulation upon a territory that remains physically limited.

## UPON A FINITE BASE

The Earth does not accumulate only technology. It also accumulates:

- pressure;
- infrastructure;
- energy demand;
- temperature;
- costs;
- irreversibilities.

The base remains the same. The additions grow ever larger.

## MORE SYSTEM UPON THE SAME BASE

### does not make the base infinite

## THE DECISIVE QUESTION

The question is no longer merely:

### What new capacity are we incorporating?

The decisive question becomes:

## What ceases to be done when the new appears?

## IF NOTHING DECREASES, EVERYTHING ACCUMULATES.
