# TOTAL SPAIN

## Total World Cup · Total eclipse · Total climate emergency

## These are not three separate scenes.

The World Cup has placed Spain under global attention. The eclipse offers the next extraordinary event. The climate emergency does not appear in the background: it is the territorial condition within which the spectacle is organised.

We do not know what will happen on 12 August or where. We do know what is already happening and the conditions under which the event is being prepared.

*The implicit advertisement might read as follows:*

## Come and enjoy one of the greatest astronomical spectacles of the century.

To reach your clear observation site, you will have to overcome various obstacles:

- 🚆 travel on trains that may stop or lose their air conditioning;
- 🚗 cross roads that are congested, closed or diverted;
- 🌡️ endure extreme temperatures and nights without recovery;
- 🔥 contend with extreme fire risk, smoke and possible forest restrictions;
- 🏞️ find shade and water in an increasingly dry territory;
- 👥 share rural roads with an influx whose full extent nobody will know until afterwards.

Once you have passed all the tests, you will be able to watch the Moon cover the Sun for a few minutes.

## Afterwards, you will have to find a way back.
