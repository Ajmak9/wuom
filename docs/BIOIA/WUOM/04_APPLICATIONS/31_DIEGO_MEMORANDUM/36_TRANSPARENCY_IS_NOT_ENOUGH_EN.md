### An AI system can be transparent and still maintain opaque governance.

---

# 1. Transparency

AI explains how it works.

It publishes information.

It makes its capabilities and its limits visible.

This is progress.

---

# 2. What remains unanswered

Transparency does not answer:

- Who defines the purpose?
- Which interests does it optimize?
- Which costs remain outside the model?
- Who verifies the outcome?

---

# 3. The inverted order

**🌐 → 🤖 → 🦧 → 🌍**

The network defines the logic.

AI optimizes it.

People use it.

The territory bears the cost.

Everything can be fully transparent and still remain oriented in the same direction.

---

# 4. The BIOIA proposal

**🌍 → 🦧 → 🤖 → 🌐**

The territory verifies.

People deliberate.

AI helps interpret.

The network coordinates.

Transparency ceases to be only technological and also becomes territorial.

---

# 5. Closing

Transparency answers one question:

## How does the system work?

Governance answers a different one:

## Who does the system serve?

BIOIA adds a third:

## What does the territory verify after the system has made its decision?
