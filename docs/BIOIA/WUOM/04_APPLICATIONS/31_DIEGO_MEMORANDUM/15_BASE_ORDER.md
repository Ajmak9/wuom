# BASE ORDER

## First, what sustains

Until now, BIOIA had named the alternative to the inverted order **coexistence in continuity**. The proposal does not change. It becomes more precise.

Coexistence in continuity describes the possible relationship among territory, life, people, institutions, artificial intelligence and the network. **Base order** names the material rule that makes that relationship possible: first, what sustains; then, what depends upon it.

# 0 is not the first step. It is the ground beneath every step.

The real dependency is simple:

- The planet sustains the conditions for life and habitability.
- People sustain institutions, infrastructure and technical systems.
- The network depends on both: it can read, connect, coordinate and amplify, but it does not sustain the planet.

## Organising what is dependent from what sustains it.

## 1 · THE DEPENDENCY OMITTED BY THE NARRATIVE

# What sustains what?  
# Who extracts from where?  
# Who can continue without whom?

The network does not itself produce the energy it consumes, the water it needs, the materials from which it is built, the bodies that maintain it or the territory where it is installed.

Its appearance of autonomy depends on visually separating the technological layer from the material base that sustains it. What is above cannot support itself. Hiding the ground makes it possible to pretend that it can.

| LAYER | WHAT IT CONTRIBUTES | WHAT IT DEPENDS ON |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PLANET / TERRITORY** | Soil, water, energy, materials, living cycles and conditions of habitability. | Can continue without the network. |
| **PEOPLE AND COMMUNITIES** | Work, knowledge, care, maintenance, legitimacy and decision-making. | Cannot continue without habitable conditions. |
| **INSTITUTIONS AND INFRASTRUCTURE** | Organisation, services, rules and collective capacities. | Depend on people and territory. |
| **NETWORK AND AI** | Reading, connection, calculation, coordination and amplification. | Depend on all the preceding layers. |

---

## 2 · THE INVERTED ORDER

The current order inverts material dependency:

# The network defines the purpose  
# → people, institutions and AI accelerate  
# → the territory supplies resources and absorbs the cost

The problem is not the existence of the network, AI or infrastructure. The problem arises when a dependent structure occupies the purpose of the system and treats what sustains it as an available resource, passive support or place in which to discharge consequences.

The ground appears when it can be extracted, occupied, drilled, connected or used to absorb damage. It disappears when it should establish conditions and limits.

## 3 · CHROMATIC TRANSITION

### Changing the colour without changing the order

Chromatic transition does not replace one architecture with another. It adds new machinery, new networks and new infrastructure to the capacity that already exists, while preserving the same purpose of expansion and acceleration.

# The machinery does not transition: it accumulates.  
# The colour changes; the order remains.

The central paradox is the attempt to reverse the imbalance by intensifying the mechanism that produced it:

- accelerate in order to slow down;
- extract more in order to reduce extractive harm;
- expand infrastructure in order to reduce pressure;
- make limits flexible in order to protect limited conditions;
- call maintaining the same direction a transition.

# More speed in the same direction is not transition. It is chromatic acceleration.

## 4 · THE NEW TOOL AND THE TWO DIRECTIONS

# They are opposite directions:

# Destroy what remains or preserve what is still here?

The question is not what artificial intelligence can do. The question is what it is used for and within which order it is placed.

| INVERTED ORDER | BASE ORDER |
| --- | --- |
| AI optimises an already imposed purpose. | AI helps to read limits and relationships. |
| It increases the speed, scale and capacity of extraction. | It makes accumulation, imbalance and alternatives visible. |
| It manages the subsequent damage. | It does not replace human deliberation. |
| It helps to destroy what remains. | It helps to preserve what is still here. |

---

## 5 · FUNCTIONING IN EQUILIBRIUM

Equilibrium does not mean immobility, perfection or the absence of conflict. It means that each function occupies a position compatible with what it depends upon, and that no layer systematically consumes the base that enables its continuity.

- The territory establishes material conditions and limits.
- Situated experience observes, remembers and verifies.
- People and institutions deliberate and assume responsibility.
- AI helps to read patterns, relationships and consequences.
- The network coordinates without becoming the purpose.
- Return restores capacities and verifies that the relationship has not been extractive.

The current ecosystem is out of balance because the existence of a ground is ignored, except when something can be extracted from it. The dominant response manages the symptoms without repositioning the dependency relationship.

# Without return, there is extraction.  
# Without a base, the network merely shines.

## 6 · THE ROCKET AND THE LAUNCH PAD

The current direction is presented as a race towards the future: more network, more speed, more capacity and greater deployment. But it destroys the path and the material base that make progress possible.

> **It promises to leave the past behind by taking off from the Earth, while destroying the conditions necessary both to take off and for any future to exist.**

The rocket does not appear from nowhere. It needs materials, energy, water, infrastructure, knowledge, bodies, social stability and a physical base from which to depart.

# Without a launch pad, the rocket has nowhere to leave from.  
# And the only base we have is the Earth.

The network does not take off from the ground. It extracts from the ground its capacity to appear autonomous. Advancing by destroying what makes advancement possible is not progress: it is acceleration towards the loss of continuity.

## 7 · BASE ORDER: REORGANISING WHAT IS DEPENDENT

# The planet establishes conditions and limits  
# → situated experience observes and verifies  
# → people and institutions deliberate and decide  
# → AI helps to read  
# → the network coordinates  
# → return verifies the relationship

This does not mean returning to the past. Nor does it mean rejecting technology or replacing those occupying the same saucer. It means returning each function to a position compatible with its real dependency.

The network does not disappear: it ceases to occupy the purpose and begins to perform coordination. AI does not disappear: it ceases to optimise a given direction and helps to read real conditions.

---

## 8 · TWO ORDERS, TWO DIRECTIONS

| DIMENSION | INVERTED ORDER | BASE ORDER |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose | The network defines the purpose. | The territory establishes conditions; people deliberate upon the purpose. |
| AI | Optimises and accelerates an imposed direction. | Helps to read patterns, alternatives and consequences. |
| Institutions | Manage growth and impacts. | Assume limits, custody, adjustment and the capacity to stop. |
| Territory | Supplies resources and absorbs costs. | Remains the base, limit, memory and verification. |
| Return | Late, generic or non-existent compensation. | A concrete, continuous and verifiable condition. |
| Outcome | Greater operational capacity and a smaller territorial margin. | Continuity, recovery and collective capacity. |

## 9 · A COLLECTIVE CHANGE OF COURSE

No person, estate, city or country can preserve common conditions of habitability separately. The change must be collective, not because everyone must think alike, but because enough people, territories, communities and institutions must stop sustaining the wrong direction.

# Not a leader above the whole.  
# The whole exercising orientation.

The planet can continue without us. We and the network cannot continue without the planet.

# From the inverted order to the base order.
