## 01 · THE DOCUMENTED MOMENT

# We do not know why that moment was chosen.  
# We do know which moment was chosen.

### 14 JUL · PARLIAMENT

ENVI and ITRE held their final ordinary meetings before September. Both committees announced that they would return on 1 September.

### 15 JUL · HEAT ALERT

AEMET and Civil Protection warned of very high and persistent temperatures, hot and dry air, airborne dust and forecast highs of 42-44 °C, with the episode expected to continue at least until 22 July.

### 16 JUL · CLIMATE CRISIS

The European Commission commemorated the victims of the climate crisis during a summer already marked by heatwaves and fires across Europe.

### 17 JUL · TWO PROPOSALS

The Commission simultaneously presented the Electrification Action Plan and a targeted revision of the European Emissions Trading System (ETS). Civil Protection maintained the alert and stated that the episode could develop into a new heatwave.

### 19 JUL · PEAK MEDIA ATTENTION

The World Cup final between Spain and Argentina was scheduled for two days after the proposals were published.

## The intention has not been proven. The timeline has.

## 02 · BEFORE 17 JULY

# The signals were already on the table.

### HEALTH · 11 JUN

WHO Europe reported more than 200,000 heat-attributable deaths in the EU and associated countries over the previous four years. It stated that most of them were preventable.

### MORTALITY · 22-28 JUN

Santé publique France recorded an increase of nearly 30% in deaths from all causes compared with the previous week, although the figures were still provisional.

### ANIMALS · 24 JUN

The heatwave killed hundreds of thousands of poultry in France and overwhelmed carcass-removal systems in producing regions.

### RECORDS AND TERRITORY · 09 JUL

Copernicus and the WMO confirmed the hottest June on record in Western Europe, widespread dryness, increased fire risk and marine heatwaves.

### ATMOSPHERE · 15 JUL

*Nature* published research on the rise in desert-dust pollution transported across Europe. The study links recent episodes to changes in atmospheric circulation and the long-term trend to increasing aridity in North Africa.

## There was no shortage of data. No shortage of records. No shortage of warnings.

## 03 · TWO SIMULTANEOUS MOVEMENTS

# One declaration.  
# Two material directions.

| ELECTRIFICATION PLAN | ETS REVISION |
| --- | --- |
| Electrification of European demand had remained stalled at 23% for a decade. The Commission proposed an indicative target of 46% by 2040 and measures to accelerate the shift in industry, transport and buildings. | The Commission proposed slowing the annual rate at which the emissions cap contracts: 3.7% from 2031 and 1.7% from 2036, compared with the current 4.3%. It also envisages international credits equivalent to 2% of the reductions required from ETS sectors from 2036 onwards. |

## It is declared that the transition must accelerate; at the same time, one of its principal brakes is made more gradual.

The Commission presents the package as a policy of competitiveness, energy independence and decarbonisation.

In material terms, the cap would continue to fall, but more slowly and with greater operational room for industry.

## 04 · CONTRADICTION OF ORIENTATION

# The limit is acknowledged.  
# Then it is made flexible.

### DECLARED DIRECTION

The Paris Agreement sets the objective of holding the increase in temperature to well below 2 °C and pursuing efforts to limit it to 1.5 °C.

### OPERATIONAL DIRECTION

With 2030 drawing ever closer, slowing the contraction of the emissions cap is not a neutral decision: it reduces the speed of one of the EU's central mitigation instruments.

> **The proposal did not appear before the risk was known. It appeared after a public accumulation of deaths, damage, records and warnings, and proposes making more gradual the brake intended to correct that trajectory.**

| THE COMMON BASE | THE INSTRUMENT |
| --- | --- |
| It was already showing less margin: heat stress, dryness, fires, mortality and loss of regulatory capacity. | It proposes granting more temporal and operational room to the sectors covered by the ETS. |

No direct causality is claimed between the file and a heatwave, fire, death or the loss of animals. What is identified is a contradiction of orientation.

## 05 · FILE ON PAUSE

# Reality does not go into recess.

The proposal was published after the committees' final ordinary meetings, on a Friday, with the weekend about to begin, amid a new heat episode and two days before the World Cup final.

## We do not know why that moment was chosen. We do know which moment was chosen.

| THE FILE | REALITY |
| --- | --- |
| It can wait for Parliament to resume in September. The text will remain available and can be presented as a technical adjustment. | It continues to accumulate temperature, impose disruptions, register impacts on life and verify the loss of territorial capacity. |

When Parliament examines the proposal, it will be able to:

1. observe the inconsistency and correct or reject it;
2. accept it as "flexibility" and normalise a slower brake.

Neither has happened yet. The outcome is therefore not being judged: the starting point is recorded and verification remains open.

## 06 · UPDATE TO 19 JULY

# The list of signals is already longer.

### 18 JUL · THIRD HEATWAVE

Civil Protection officially announced the third heatwave of the summer, expected to begin on Tuesday 21 and continue at least until Thursday 23. Temperatures above 42 °C and local highs exceeding 45 °C were forecast in some areas.

### 19 JUL · THE CONTEXT REMAINS ACTIVE

As the proposal enters a period of reduced parliamentary activity, the heat episode continues to unfold and retains the capacity to disrupt health, work, mobility, energy, agriculture, biodiversity and infrastructure.

## In September, the text may look the same. The list of signals will not.

The inverted order is recognised not by what it declares it will protect, but by what it places first when a decision must be made.

We are not dealing with direct causality between the file and the observed impacts. We are dealing with a contradiction of orientation and an operational pattern: acknowledge the limit, make it flexible when it becomes inconvenient and then manage its consequences.
