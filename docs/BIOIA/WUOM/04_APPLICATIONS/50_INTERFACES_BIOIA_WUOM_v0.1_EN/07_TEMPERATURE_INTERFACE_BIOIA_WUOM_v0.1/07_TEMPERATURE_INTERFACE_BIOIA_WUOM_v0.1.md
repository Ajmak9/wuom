# 07_TEMPERATURE_INTERFACE_BIOIA_WUOM_v0.1

## Status

**Version:** v0.1  
**Type:** Interface synthesis  
**System:** BIOIA / WUOM  
**Function:** Interpret the thermal organization of the territory as a condition of shared habitability.

---

## Overview

The **Temperature Interface** does not focus on temperature as an isolated variable.  
It focuses on **when a thermal signal alters the territory’s capacity to sustain life**.

This interface does **not** measure temperature, run meteorology, or manage emergencies.  
Its role is to interpret **thermal habitability** as a territorial condition.

---

## Foundational question

**How can the thermal organization of the territory be incorporated into situated orientation in order to understand when a thermal signal modifies the shared habitability of the territory?**

---

## Organizing center

**Thermal habitability of the territory**

This is the interface’s organizing center.  
From this center, the interface interprets when heat stops being only a meteorological observation and becomes a territorial condition that affects life.

---

## Why this interface emerges

This interface appears because repeated territorial observation has shown:

- persistence of high temperatures;
- weaker nighttime thermal recovery;
- interaction between heat and water stress;
- simultaneous effects on people, ecosystems, and territorial activities;
- repetition of extreme heat episodes;
- difference between isolated emergencies and longer thermal trajectories.

Temperature therefore becomes more than an atmospheric datum.  
It becomes a **habitability condition**.

---

## Purpose

The Temperature Interface exists to:

- interpret thermal signals as territorial expressions;
- distinguish between thermal episodes and thermal trajectories;
- understand how heat alters shared habitability;
- relate temperature to water, biodiversity, community, time, genetics, and communication;
- support situated decisions that improve the territory’s capacity to sustain life.

Its aim is **not to react better**, but to **orient better**.

---

## Core architecture

The interface is organized through three sequential modules:

```text
Thermal Reading
        ↓
Thermo-territorial Integration
        ↓
Situated Orientation through Thermal Habitability
````

### 1. Thermal Reading

Interprets the thermal signal as a territorial expression.

It reads:

* persistence;
* accumulation;
* recovery;
* exposed surfaces and bodies;
* ecosystems under thermal stress;
* continuity of the signal over time.

### 2. Thermo-territorial Integration

Relates the thermal signal to other territorial dimensions.

It integrates temperature with:

* **Water** — soil moisture, evaporation, water stress;
* **Biodiversity** — shade, vegetation cover, ecological resilience;
* **Community** — thermal vulnerability, shared spaces, social infrastructure;
* **Genetics** — biological adaptation to thermal conditions;
* **Communication** — custody of orientation and public interpretation;
* **Time** — continuity, recovery, accumulation, trajectory.

### 3. Situated Orientation

Transforms thermal habitability into territorial orientation.

It helps orient decisions toward:

* reducing the alteration of habitability;
* decreasing repetition of thermal emergencies;
* strengthening ecological infrastructure;
* improving the territory’s capacity to sustain life;
* modifying thermal trajectories in the medium and long term.

---

## From episode to trajectory

A key contribution of this interface is distinguishing between:

* **Episode** — a specific thermal emergency;
* **Persistence** — the signal repeats and remains;
* **Trajectory** — a lasting territorial thermal pattern over time.

This distinction shifts attention away from one-off reactions and toward structural interpretation.

---

## What this interface contributes to BIOIA / WUOM

The Temperature Interface adds a specific dimension to the system:

> **thermal habitability**

It does not replace other interfaces.
It complements them by showing how the thermal signal modifies the conditions that allow life to persist.

---

## Relation with the system

The Temperature Interface:

* operates within the existing BIOIA/WUOM architecture;
* preserves methodological continuity with the other interfaces;
* does not alter the core system;
* does not introduce a new cognitive architecture;
* validates orientation through the **Law of Territorial Return**.

### Law of Territorial Return

Every thermal orientation must be contrasted through territorial return.

Without return, there is no validation.
Without validation, there is no orientation.

---

## What it is not

This interface is **not**:

* meteorology;
* climatology;
* sensor monitoring;
* thermal engineering;
* emergency management;
* prediction;
* atmospheric modeling;
* public policy prescription.

It **interprets habitability**.
It does **not measure**, **predict**, or **manage**.

---

## Foundational principle

> **Temperature does not matter in itself.
> It matters because it modifies the possibility that the territory can sustain life.**

---

## Epistemic status

**v0.1 is foundational and provisional.**

It may evolve only through:

* situated practice;
* territorial return;
* verified evidence;
* contained integration with other interfaces.

One methodological hypothesis remains open:

> BIOIA/WUOM may eventually incorporate interfaces organized around **conditions of territorial habitability**.

At v0.1, this is **not yet considered demonstrated**.

---

## Closing note

The Temperature Interface formalizes a territorial reading organ devoted to **thermal habitability**.

It emerges from repeated territorial evidence rather than theoretical completeness.

Its role is clear:

* read the thermal signal,
* integrate it with the territory,
* return orientation.

---

**Without custody there is no publication.**
**Without return there is no orientation.**
