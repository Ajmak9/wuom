## Purpose

This folder documents **Territorial Geometry and Irregular Polygons** as a derived Territorial GIS application inside BIOIA / WUOM.

Its function is to describe how irregular territorial forms can be read not as simple geometric errors, but as situated traces of relief, land use, history, fragmentation, decision and territorial pressure.

This is not a foundational document.
It does not redefine BIOIA / WUOM.
It does not open respiration.
It does not activate WUOM as a general phase.

## Operational Definition

An irregular polygon is a closed geometry whose sides, angles, borders or proportions do not follow a regular form.

In GIS, irregular polygons can represent:

```text
parcels
farms
urban zones
agricultural areas
watersheds
protected areas
municipal limits
risk zones
land use patches
influence areas
fragmented territories
environmental boundaries
```

Irregularity is not automatically an error.

It can also be a territorial signal.

## Regular Polygon vs Territorial Polygon

```text
Regular polygon:
stable form
predictable borders
simple proportion
abstract reading

Irregular territorial polygon:
situated form

```

The real territory rarely appears as pure geometry.

## Technical Risks

Irregular polygons can generate errors if they are not reviewed correctly.

Common risks include:

```text
invalid geometries
unclosed rings
unintended overlaps
internal gaps
duplicated vertices
self-intersecting borders
projection errors
incorrect area calculations
excessive simplification
loss of territorial detail
```

Not every irregular geometry is wrong.

But every irregular geometry requires careful reading.
