## Purpose

This folder documents **Environmental Assessment and Territorial Decision** as a derived Territorial GIS application inside BIOIA / WUOM.

Its function is to describe how environmental information can be organized, assessed and oriented before acting on a territory.

This is not a foundational document.
It does not redefine BIOIA / WUOM.
It does not open respiration.
It does not activate WUOM as a general phase.

## Operational Definition

Environmental assessment allows a territorial action, project, use or transformation to be read before intervention.

It can involve relations between:

```text
soil
water
vegetation
relief
biodiversity
infrastructure
human activity
risk
land use
territorial memory
public or private decision
```

Its purpose is not only to measure impacts.

Its purpose is to understand what relations may be altered, interrupted, intensified or regenerated.

---

## BIOIA / WUOM Reading

```text
BIOIA = living territorial field

GIS = system that organizes environmental and spatial layers

Environmental assessment = reading of impacts, tensions and conditions

WUOM = apparatus that orients relations before deciding
```

Environmental assessment does not only classify damage.

It reads the relation between intervention, territory and consequence.

---

## Assessment Is Not Accumulation

More layers do not automatically produce a better decision.

```text
more layers
≠
better decision
```

The operational question is:

```text
Which layers are relevant?
Which relations appear between them?
Which territorial tension do they reveal?
Which decision can they help orient?
```

Environmental assessment must filter, relate and situate information.

```text
vulnerability
reversibility
social impact
ecological impact
regeneration capacity
territorial memory
```

This layer avoids reducing environmental assessment to:

Environmental assessment must be situated reading.

Not only formal compliance.

---

## Technical Risks

Environmental assessment can fail because of:

```text
outdated data
inadequate scale
incomplete layers
incorrect projection
undeclared criteria
excessive simplification
absence of field observation
disconnection from local communities
confusing risk with impact
ignoring cumulative effects
```

The result may look technical while remaining poorly situated.

---

## Operational Rule

Before taking a territorial decision based on environmental assessment:

```text
verify layers
verify scale
verify source
verify temporality
verify vulnerabilities
verify accumulated relations
verify indirect impacts
verify reversibility
verify relation with human and non-human inhabitants
```

The final question is not only:

```text
Can it be done?
```

The correct question is:

```text
What does it transform, what does it compromise and what remains viable?
```

## BIOIA Reading of Decision

In BIOIA / WUOM, deciding does not mean imposing a solution on the territory.

Deciding means responding to a reading.

```text
The territory shows conditions.
GIS organizes signals.
WUOM reads relations.
The decision must respect what the reading reveals.
```

A weak decision ignores relations.

A situated decision recognizes limits, tensions and possibilities.
