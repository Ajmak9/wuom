## Overview

**Annex 02 — Field Response** is an applied territorial reading layer within the **BIOIA / WUOM Territorial GIS Atlas v1** and **Película Territorial v1**.

It extends the climate-based reading of **Annex 01** toward direct field response.

```text
Annex 01
= measured climate signal

Annex 02
= observed field response
```

This annex focuses on:

* vegetation
* soil cover
* invisible subsurface processes
* traces
* excrements
* insects
* path edges
* trophic activity
* repeated field observation
* paths as reading transects

It is not a theoretical layer.

It is not part of RSI.

It is an applied, situated and protected field-reading annex.

---

## Core Function

The function of Annex 02 is to show how the territory responds after climatic pressure and hydric recharge.

```text
Temperature shows pressure.

Rainfall shows recharge.

Vegetation shows response.

Traces show activity.

The territory verifies.
```

Annex 02 does not replace climate data.

It does not replace GIS analysis.

It adds a situated field layer.

---

## Methodological Direction

Many territorial readings begin from above:

```text
satellite
↓
map
↓
area
↓
field interpretation
```

BIOIA / WUOM can also use zoom, GIS and satellite reading.

However, this annex begins from inside the field:

```text
trace
↓
soil
↓
vegetation
↓
path
↓
field
↓
zoom
↓
GIS / satellite
↓
return to field
```

This is not a rejection of satellite reading.

It is a complementary direction of entry.

```text
The satellite reads the surface.

The field shows metabolism.

The trace reveals what the surface does not say.
```

## What This Annex Observes

Annex 02 observes:

* field vegetation as territorial response
* marginal flowering as edge signal
* dry olive-grove context
* soil cover as a change in reading method
* subsurface processes through visible effects
* traces as indirect territorial writing
* excrements as trophic indicators
* paths as transects, corridors and reading surfaces
* nocturnal or evening activity through visible evidence
* relations between rain, vegetation, insects, fauna and traces

---

## What This Annex Does Not Claim

This annex does not claim:

* taxonomic certainty without evidence
* species identification from isolated traces
* complete ecological validation
* predictive modelling
* GIS model validation
* climate model validation
* public access routes
* private coordinates
* vulnerable habitat locations
* exposure of sensitive territorial points

The reading remains cautious, situated and protected.
