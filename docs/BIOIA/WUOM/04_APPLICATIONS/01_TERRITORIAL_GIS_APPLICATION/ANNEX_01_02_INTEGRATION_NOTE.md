## 1. Purpose

This note defines the relationship between **Annex 01 — Climate Territorial Synthesis** and **Annex 02 — Field Response** within the **BIOIA / WUOM Territorial GIS Atlas v1** and **Película Territorial v1**.

Its purpose is to clarify the transition from measured climate data to observed territorial response.

```text
Annex 01
= measured climate signal

Annex 02
= observed field response
```

---

## 2. Annex 01 — Measured Climate Signal

Annex 01 works with official climate data.

It focuses on:

* temperature
* precipitation
* annual comparison
* partial-year interpretation
* thermal pressure
* hydric recharge
* climate sequence

Its function is to establish the climatic context.

It shows how the territory enters a given seasonal phase in terms of heat and water.

---

## 3. Annex 02 — Observed Field Response

Annex 02 works with situated field observation.

It focuses on:

* vegetation
* soil cover
* path edges
* traces
* excrements
* insects
* trophic signals
* repeated observation
* paths as reading transects

Its function is to observe how the territory responds after the climatic signal.

It does not replace Annex 01.

It extends it toward the field.

---

## 4. Integration Logic

The relationship between both annexes can be read as:

```text
climate
↓
water
↓
vegetation
↓
soil
↓
insects
↓
traces
↓
territorial reading
```

Annex 01 shows pressure and recharge.

Annex 02 shows response and activity.

Together, they create a complete applied reading sequence.

---

## 5. Methodological Difference

Annex 01 is based on measured external reference data.

Annex 02 begins from inside the field.

```text
Annex 01
= external climatic reference

Annex 02
= internal field reading
```

BIOIA / WUOM does not reject satellite, GIS or external data.

It completes them through situated observation.

```text
The satellite reads the surface.

The field shows metabolism.

The trace reveals what the surface does not say.
```

---

## 6. Territorial Reading Formula

```text
Temperature shows pressure.

Rainfall shows recharge.

Vegetation shows response.

Traces show activity.

The territory verifies.
```

This formula links both annexes into one applied territorial sequence.

---

## 7. Protection Clause

This integration note does not expose:

* private coordinates
* sensitive access routes
* vulnerable field points
* water infrastructure
* wildlife refuges
* exploitable territorial patterns
* full internal methodology

Its function is to explain the relationship between annexes without exposing the protected internal field layer.

```text
Registering does not imply exposing.
```

---

## 8. Relation to Película Territorial v1

Within **Película Territorial v1**, the annexes work as consecutive scenes.

```text
Annex 01
= climate scene

Annex 02
= field response scene
```

The territorial film does not move only through data.

It moves through territorial response.

---

## 9. Closing Formula

```text
The field is not the end of the data.

It is where the data touches ground again.
```
