## Purpose

This folder documents **Socioecological Territory** as a derived territorial GIS application inside BIOIA / WUOM.

It describes territory not only as a physical, measurable or ecological space, but as a living field of relations between:

```text
nature
community
memory
water
institutions
situated knowledge
governance
territorial care
```

This is not a foundational document.
It does not redefine BIOIA / WUOM.
It does not open respiration.
It does not activate WUOM as a general phase.

## Operational Definition

A socioecological territory is not formed only by natural ecosystems.

It is formed by permanent interaction between:

```text
people
histories
practices
knowledge
institutions
infrastructures
water
soil
biodiversity
conflicts
forms of care
```

The territory is not a sum of layers.

It is a living network of relations.

---

## BIOIA / WUOM Reading

```text
BIOIA = living field where nature, community, memory and institutions interact

GIS = system that organizes physical, social and territorial layers

WUOM = apparatus that reads relations, tensions and interdependencies

Situated inhabitant / pagès = living presence that sustains local territorial reading
```

The socioecological layer prevents territory from being reduced to a measurable surface or a technical dataset.

---

## What This Layer Corrects

This layer avoids reducing:

```text
territory to map
community to data
nature to resource
risk to isolated variable
water to infrastructure
governance to administration
memory to secondary information
```

Territory becomes legible when its living relations are read together.

```text
LiDAR

```

It adds a specific question:

```text
Who inhabits the territory?
What memories sustain it?
Which institutions organize it?
What conflicts cross it?
What practices care for it?
What human and non-human relations configure it?
```

---

## Operational Rule

Before acting on a territory, its socioecological relations must be read.

```text
It is not enough to know where the layers are.
It is necessary to understand what relations sustain, tension or damage the territory.
```

Territorial reading does not end in visualization.

It becomes useful when it helps understand how nature, people, institutions and decisions coexist.
