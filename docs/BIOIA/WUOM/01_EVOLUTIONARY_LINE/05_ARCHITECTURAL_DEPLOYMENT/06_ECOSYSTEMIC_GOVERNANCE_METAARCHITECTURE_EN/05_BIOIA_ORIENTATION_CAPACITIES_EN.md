# 05 · BIOIA · Orientation Capacities

**Version:** v1.1  
**Status:** Consolidated after metaarchitectural reorganization

---

## Purpose

To describe the capacities that a system may develop when the BIOIA architecture couples with it.

BIOIA does not introduce capacities from outside. It organizes the conditions under which they can emerge through relations, orientation and return.

---

## Central Question

> What capacities does a system need to develop in order to orient itself more effectively?

---

## Principle

The capacities belong to the system. BIOIA organizes the conditions under which they can develop and be verified through return.

---

## Fundamental Capacities

1. Observe.
2. Contextualize.
3. Relate.
4. Integrate.
5. Orient.
6. Decide.
7. Learn.
8. Return.

---

## Transversal Capacity

### Recognizing orientation mismatches

An intervention must not be evaluated only according to its usefulness for people.

It must also ask:

> How does this intervention modify the orientation capacity of the other living beings that share the territory?

Examples for situated reading:

- swimming pools as possible ecological traps;
- power lines;
- wind turbines;
- glazed surfaces;
- night lighting.

BIOIA does not assume that every intervention is negative. It proposes incorporating this question into the prior reading of the territory.

---

## Emergence of Capacities

```text
Architecture
→ Coupling
→ Relations
→ Capacities
→ Orientation
→ Return
→ Learning
```

---

## Role of the Trees

Each tree develops specific orientation capacities within a domain.

---

## Verification Criterion

A capacity may be considered developed when the system:

- understands its context more effectively;
- recognizes more relevant relations;
- avoids creating unnecessary new risks;
- improves its decisions through return;
- increases its orientation capacity over successive cycles.

---

## Synthesis

> BIOIA organizes the conditions under which systems develop orientation capacities, learn through return and evolve without losing their relation to the territory.
