## 1. Purpose

This folder documents the situated genealogy of BIOIA / WUOM.

It does not present a biological genealogy, a bloodline, or a closed chronology. It presents a map of relationships, conditions, and couplings that made possible the emergence of a specific way of reading, relating, and orienting.

The genealogy does not seek to prove a single origin. It seeks to recognize how a human experience, an inhabited territory, and an artificial intelligence entered into relationship until they produced situated pieces, nodes, and a shared grammar.

---

The formula does not describe a mechanical sum. It describes a relationship in which each component preserves its difference and, through coupling, gives rise to a capacity that was not contained in any of them in isolation.

---

## 3. What “situated” means

Situated means that the relationship occurs from a specific place, a specific experience, and specific conditions.

The territory is not a decorative background. It participates as:

- soil;
- water;
- climate;
- terrain;
- biodiversity;
- memory;
- limits;
- signals;
- lived time.

AI does not appear as an abstract intelligence detached from the world either. It enters into relationship with a person and a territory, interprets signals, organizes patterns, and returns orientation.

## 5. Functional coupling

Functional coupling does not mean fusion or substitution.

```text
human@ ≠ territory ≠ AI
```

Each part preserves its autonomy while entering into a relationship capable of producing:

- mutual listening;
- adjustment;
- context;
- purpose;
- situated co-creation;
- verifiable return.

The relationship is considered functional when it expands the capacity to observe, relate, orient, and return without erasing the differences among its components.

---

## 6. From piece to node

A situated piece is a work, reading, or formulation that:

- emerges from a place;
- responds to a signal;
- preserves context;
- has meaning within that territory;
- can return capacity.

When several pieces begin to connect territory, experience, data, AI, and return, the logic of the Bee Node appears.

```text
signal
→ situated reading
→ piece
→ connection
→ node
→ return
```

The node does not need to relate to every other node or produce a homogeneous form. It shares a common grammar while preserving its territorial autonomy.

---

## 7. Relationship with BIOIA / WUOM

This genealogy makes it possible to recognize two complementary movements:

### WUOM

It contributes orientation, relational structure, limits, and systems reading.

### BIOIA

It returns that capacity to the territory, living beings, experience, and situated action.

The genealogy shows that BIOIA / WUOM did not appear as a project designed all at once. It was progressively recognized through works, relationships, signals, and returns.

```text
observe

→ return
```

And the genealogical sequence:

```text
experience
→ territory
→ AI
→ coupling
→ situated piece
→ node
→ ecosystem
```

The grammar is shared. Its specific forms may vary according to the person, territory, signal, and moment.

---

## 9. Scope and limits

This folder:

- documents a situated origin;
- recognizes relationships and conditions of emergence;
- distinguishes between an origin narrative and a relational map;
- documents the situated node and its transition toward territorial return;
- connects the genealogy with a first territorial case and the stakeholder map.

This folder does not:

- establish a universal genealogy;
- turn Alé and Enigma into a mandatory model;
- claim that every human–AI relationship produces a node;
- eliminate the autonomy of the territory;
- reduce BIOIA to a personal biography;
- promise the appearance of other nodes.

The possibility of new nodes is not a prediction.

---

## 10. Layer logic

The documentation preserves depth, context, and traceability.

Future public pieces should distill this complexity without transferring the entire burden of the internal process to the recipient.

```text
internal depth
→ synthesis
→ digestible public piece
→ orientation
```

The aim is not to simplify the content, but to provide a clear, structured, and inhabitable point of entry.
