The network can locate a species, assign coordinates to it, delimit protected areas, and record each project separately.

But locating is not understanding.

Industry, infrastructure, irrigation, agricultural intensification, compensation measures, and protected areas arrive through different administrative files.

The territory does not receive separate files.
It receives effects that accumulate on the same functional foundation.

Assessing each intervention in isolation may leave out the transformation of the whole: habitat, prey, water, soil, dryland farming, ecological corridors, and communities.
