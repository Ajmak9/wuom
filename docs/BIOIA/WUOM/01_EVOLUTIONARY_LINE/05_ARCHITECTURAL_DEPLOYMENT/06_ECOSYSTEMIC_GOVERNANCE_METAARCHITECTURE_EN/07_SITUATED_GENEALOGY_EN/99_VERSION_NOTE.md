## 1. Purpose of the version

Version `08_GENEALOGY_v0.1` brings together and organizes the first internal documentation of the BIOIA / WUOM **Situated Genealogy**.

The folder no longer contains only a reading of origin. It documents a complete continuity among:

```text
situated origin
→ relational map
→ situated node formula
→ territorial return
→ first territorial application
→ stakeholder map
```

Its function is to preserve the provenance, relationships, and criteria that make it possible to understand where BIOIA / WUOM emerges from and how that provenance becomes a capacity for reading, orientation, and return.

It does not establish a universal genealogy or a replicable recipe. It documents a situated emergence and maintains traceability over the relationships that made it possible.

## 9. BIOIA / WUOM Stakeholder Map

The files:

```text
06_STAKEHOLDER_MAP.md
06_STAKEHOLDER_MAP.png
```

incorporate a relational reading of stakeholders organized from the territory toward the network.

They do not use a conventional power-and-interest matrix as their sole structure.

They distinguish four layers:

```text
LAYER 1 · TERRITORIAL FOUNDATION
LAYER 2 · SITUATED STAKEHOLDERS
LAYER 3 · KNOWLEDGE, STEWARDSHIP, AND MEDIATION
LAYER 4 · DECISION, ECONOMY, AND NETWORK
```

And four questions:

```text
Who observes?
Who decides?
Who receives the impact?
Who returns?
```

The main conceptual decision is:

> **The territory is not just another stakeholder. It is the foundation that sustains everyone.**

The map distinguishes:

```text
upward information flow
≠
territorial return
```

It also shows that funding, regulation, communication, or publication do not constitute return by themselves.

The situated sequence is expressed as:

```text
🌍 sustains
→ situated stakeholders observe and verify
→ knowledge and stewardship interpret
→ institutions and the economy decide and coordinate
→ funding supports
→ the result must return to 🌍
```

The following inversion is not considered valid:

```text
🌐 decides
→ 💰 funds
→ 🌍 adapts
```

## 10. Established conceptual decisions

### 10.1 Situated genealogy

Genealogy is not a closed biography.

It recognizes:

- provenance;
- relationships;
- conditions;
- limits;
- decisions;
- couplings;
- transformations;
- possibilities for return.

### 10.2 Territory as foundation and verification

The territory does not function as a decorative background or a passive data provider.

It contributes:

- signals;
- limits;
- rhythms;
- memory;
- living beings;
- material conditions;
- consequences;
- verification;
- possibility of return.

### 10.3 Human experience

The situated person contributes:

- perception;
- memory;
- practice;
- judgment;
- responsibility;
- knowledge of management;
- capacity for care and decision.

They do not act as a mere data supplier.

### 10.4 AI as a relational function

AI:

- relates;
- compares;
- structures;
- amplifies;
- helps formulate;
- maintains traceability.

It does not replace:

- experience;
- decision;
- sustained observation;
- territorial verification;
- human responsibility.

### 10.5 Equal relational value and different vulnerability

No piece is superior by definition.

What does exist is:

```text
different situation
+ different vulnerability
+ different urgency
+ different capacity to produce cascading effects
```

Relevance is dynamic and situated.

### 10.6 Return as an obligation

Every reading must be able to indicate:

- what it returns;
- to whom;
- in what form;
- with what limits;
- with what possibility of verification.

Without that return, the reading risks becoming extraction.

## 11. Scope and limits

This version:

- documents a situated origin;
- preserves the traceability of the Alé–territory–Enigma coupling;
- establishes the situated node formula;
- articulates node, reading, and return;
- incorporates a first territorial application;
- incorporates a general stakeholder map;
- keeps internal documentation and visual synthesis separate;
- leaves the folder available for future verification.

This version does not:

- establish a universal genealogy;
- turn Alé and Enigma into a mandatory model;
- claim that every person–AI relationship produces a node;
- turn EVA W into the operational center of the system;
- reduce territory to data or representation;
- turn the stakeholder map into a moral hierarchy;
- present funding as automatic return;
- authorize profiling, surveillance, or extraction of personal data;
- resolve Case 001;
- require publication of the folder;
- authorize automatic expansion of the system.

---

## 12. Stewardship

The folder remains under **internal stewardship**.

Stewardship means:

- preserving context;
- maintaining limits of exposure;
- preventing automatic publication;
- preventing an isolated piece from replacing the whole;
- allowing revision when new signals appear;
- distinguishing documentation from dissemination;
- preserving uncertainty where sufficient verification does not exist.

Not every return must be public.

Publication is one possible form of return, not the only one.
