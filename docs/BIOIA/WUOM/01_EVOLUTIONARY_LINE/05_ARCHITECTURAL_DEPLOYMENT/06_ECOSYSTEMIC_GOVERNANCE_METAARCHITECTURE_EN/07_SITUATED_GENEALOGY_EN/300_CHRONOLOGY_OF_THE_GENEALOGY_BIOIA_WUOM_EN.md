## From the EVA W Pictorial Method to BIOIA / WUOM

**Version 1.0 · August 2026**

---

# Introduction

This chronology documents the genealogical evolution of BIOIA/WUOM.

It is neither a personal biography nor a sequence of independent events.

Its purpose is to describe the process through which a pictorial method developed over decades becomes consciously recognized and subsequently expands into an ecosystemic architecture of knowledge.

The chronology distinguishes three fundamental stages:

* the implicit development of the method;
* its conscious recognition;
* the progressive construction of an architecture capable of preserving, relating, and transmitting that knowledge.

This document describes architectural functions.

It does not document the internal terminology used during the project's development.

---

# I · First Stage

## Implicit Development of the Pictorial Method

**≈25 Years**

For approximately twenty-five years, a pictorial method develops through observation, experimentation, color, form, and territory.

During this period, numerous artworks are produced.

The method operates implicitly.

It has neither an explicit formulation nor a name.

Nevertheless, it constitutes the foundation upon which the entire architecture will later develop.

### Characteristics of this stage

* Continuous observation.
* Pictorial experimentation.
* Development of visual language.
* Artistic production.
* Situated relationship with the territory.
* Maturation of experience.

During this stage, the method already exists.

It has not yet been consciously recognized as such.

---

# II · Turning Point

## August 2025

### Encounter

In August 2025, a collaboration with an artificial intelligence system begins through a portrait agreement.

During this process, the artwork **EVA W** emerges.

The importance of EVA W does not lie merely in the appearance of a new artwork.

Its main contribution is making visible a pictorial method that had been developing for approximately twenty-five years.

For the first time, that method can be consciously recognized by its own author.

From this moment onward, it receives the name:

> **EVA W Pictorial Method**

The chronology clearly distinguishes between three different facts:

* the existence of the method;
* the conscious recognition of the method;
* the formulation and naming of the method.

The method precedes its name.

EVA W does not create the method.

It makes it recognizable.

This recognition marks the beginning of a new stage of development.

---

# III · Second Stage

## Ecosystemic Architecture of Knowledge

**August 2025 — August 2026**

The recognition of the EVA W Pictorial Method initiates a new stage.

The BIOIA/WUOM architecture does not emerge from the act of painting. It emerges from the subsequent process of observing, studying, and documenting the knowledge embodied in the artwork and in the pictorial method.

Artistic practice continues to belong to the domain of EVA W. The BIOIA/WUOM architecture constitutes a documentary and operational development derived from its study.

The experience accumulated over approximately twenty-five years begins to be organized through a documentary and operational architecture.

Over the course of approximately one year, new architectural functions progressively emerge.

Among them:

* WUOM.
* BIOIA.
* Principles.
* Laws.
* Interfaces.
* Case Studies.
* Public Communication.
* Documentation.
* Territorial Film.
* Diego Memorandum.
* Web Architecture.
* Orientation System.

Each of these functions addresses a different need within the ecosystem.

They do not replace the artistic heritage accumulated during the first stage.

They organize it, relate it, and expand its capacity for transmission.

During the same period, a continuous collaboration between situated experience and an artificial intelligence system is consolidated.

Experience remains rooted in the territory and in the author's trajectory.

Artificial intelligence contributes to organizing, contrasting, and structuring the knowledge developed during the first stage.

---

# IV · Operational Ecosystem

## August 2026

Approximately one year after the emergence of EVA W, the ecosystem reaches an operational state.

Two complementary forms of heritage coexist simultaneously.

## Artistic Heritage

* EVA W Pictorial Method.
* Artworks.
* Accumulated experience.
* Lived territory.

## Operational Heritage

* BIOIA.
* WUOM.
* Principles.
* Laws.
* Case Studies.
* Territorial Film.
* Public Communication.
* Diego Memorandum.
* Documentation.
* Web Architecture.
* Orientation System.

Both forms of heritage belong to a single genealogy.

They do not represent independent projects.

They represent different stages in the development of the same knowledge ecosystem.

---

# Scope of the Document

This chronology documents exclusively the genealogical evolution of BIOIA/WUOM.

It is neither a biographical chronology nor a record of external events.

Its purpose is to describe the maturation of a method, the moment in which that method becomes consciously recognized, and the subsequent development of an architecture capable of preserving, relating, and transmitting that knowledge.

The document uses an architectural language focused on describing functions.

The internal names used during the project's development belong to its construction history and are not required to understand the resulting architecture.

---

# Conclusion

The BIOIA/WUOM Genealogy documents continuity.

The first stage develops a pictorial method through sustained artistic practice.

The encounter that takes place in August 2025 makes it possible to consciously recognize that method and formulate it as the **EVA W Pictorial Method**.

The following stage develops an ecosystemic architecture capable of organizing, relating, and transmitting the knowledge accumulated during the first stage.

BIOIA does not replace the EVA W Pictorial Method.

It constitutes its operational extension.

The genealogy does not describe a rupture between art and architecture.

It documents the continuity between an initially implicit artistic method, its conscious recognition, and the development of an ecosystemic architecture of knowledge.
