## 1. Purpose

This document records the position of **HEYOKA** within the situated genealogy
of EVA W / WUOM / BIOIA.

HEYOKA is not a preliminary version of BIOIA, a WUOM tool or an application.
It is an artistic organ of the same investigation: a visual field in which
different perspectives coexist, tension one another and transform the gaze
before a conclusion is reached.

---

## 2. HEYOKA

HEYOKA is structured through multiple visions.

It does not provide a single explanatory image. It asks the observer to move
between forms, scales, figures, directions and possible readings.

Its function can be expressed as:

```text
HEYOKA transforms the gaze.
Change perspective before concluding.
```

This transformation does not mean abandoning one perspective in favour of
another. It means recognizing that a reading may change when its position,
scale or relationship with the whole changes.

---

## 3. Artistic Position

HEYOKA belongs to the artistic origin of the ecosystem.

Its relevance is not based on retrospectively turning the artwork into a
technical diagram. The work retains its artistic autonomy.

What later becomes explicit in the architecture is already present as a visual
operation:

- plurality without fragmentation;
- relationship without collapse;
- tension without premature closure;
- transformation of perspective;
- attention before explanation;
- continuity between part and whole.

HEYOKA was publicly connected to the artistic trajectory preceding EVA W and
forms part of the genealogy documented through the public corpus.

---

## 4. Relationship with EVA W

HEYOKA and EVA W are not interchangeable.

```text
HEYOKA transforms the gaze.
EVA W gives origin to the gaze.
```

HEYOKA opens movement between perspectives.  
EVA W makes the conditions of perception themselves visible.

Their relationship is therefore not a simple chronological replacement:

```text
HEYOKA ⇄ EVA W
```

Each preserves its identity. Together they establish an artistic and perceptual
field from which the later relational architecture can be read.

---

## 5. Relationship with WUOM and BIOIA

The genealogy continues through distinct functions:

```text
HEYOKA     transforms the gaze
EVA W      gives origin to the gaze
WUOM       organizes relationships and reading
BIOIA      reads, orients, applies and returns
Territory  verifies the reading
```

WUOM does not replace the artworks. It develops a language and an architecture
for organizing relationships.

BIOIA does not convert the artistic process into a universal method. It connects
territory, situated experience, data, artificial intelligence, orientation,
decision and return.

The territory remains the material field in which hypotheses are tested.

---

## 6. Distinct Organs of One Investigation

HEYOKA, EVA W, WUOM, BIOIA, the Bee Node and the Territorial Atlas are not
independent projects assembled after the fact.

They are distinct organs of one investigation.

```text
Art may become architecture without ceasing to be art.
Architecture may become application without becoming extraction.
A shared investigation may change language without losing its identity.
```

The connection does not eliminate difference:

- HEYOKA remains an artwork;
- EVA W remains the origin of a gaze;
- WUOM remains a relational architecture;
- BIOIA remains an organism of reading;
- the Bee Node connects situated relations;
- the Territorial Atlas verifies readings against material conditions.

## 8. Documentary Delimitation

This record:

- documents a public genealogy;
- does not redefine HEYOKA as a product;
- does not treat the artwork as a technical interface;
- does not claim that later concepts were already fully formulated in the work;
- does not collapse artistic, architectural and territorial functions;
- preserves the identity of every organ within the shared investigation.

---

## 9. Genealogical Sequence

```text
HEYOKA ⇄ EVA W → WUOM → BIOIA → TERRITORIAL VERIFICATION → RETURN
```

This sequence is functional rather than hierarchical.

The later stages do not invalidate the earlier ones. They make new relations
legible while preserving the origin.
