# 04 · FROM THE SITUATED NODE TO TERRITORIAL RETURN

**Version:** v0.1  
**Status:** internal development document

**Date:** 2026-07-14

---

## 1. Purpose

This document articulates a continuity that was already present in the BIOIA / WUOM architecture, but until now appeared distributed across several pieces:

- **Situated Genealogy**, which makes it possible to recognize the position from which the person entering observes and acts;
- the **Situated Node**, which makes it possible to locate the specific piece from which the reading begins;
- **Territorial Return**, which makes it possible to identify what understanding, orientation, or formulation returns to the territory and those who inhabit it.

The relationship among these three dimensions can be expressed as follows:

> **Genealogy situates the observer; the node situates the piece through which one enters; return situates what goes back to the territory.**

The aim is not to create a hierarchy, a chain of command, or an organization of functions. BIOIA / WUOM does not allocate positions. It enables situated relationships to be read.

---

## 2. General sequence

```text
🌳📍 Situated genealogy
From where do I observe and act?

        ↓

🐝📍🧩 Situated node
Through which piece, place, and moment do I enter?

        ↓

OBSERVE → RELATE → UNDERSTAND → ORIENT

        ↓

🧑‍🌾📍🪧 Situated return
What returns to the territory, for whom, and in what form?
```

This sequence should not be understood as a rigid or linear process. There may be partial returns, new observations, revisions, and re-entry through other pieces.

Return does not necessarily close the reading. It may open a new one.

---

## 3. Situated genealogy

Situated Genealogy makes it possible to recognize that no reading begins from a neutral point.

The observer enters with:

- a history;
- an experience;
- a relationship with the territory;
- prior learning;
- family and community bonds;
- decisions;
- limits;
- resources;
- conflicts;
- questions;
- forms of perception.

Genealogy does not rigidly determine what a person can understand or do. It situates the conditions from which that understanding begins.

Its main question is:

> **From where does the observer enter?**

Genealogy provides context for the reading, but it does not replace territorial verification.

---

## 4. Situated node

A Situated Node is a specific entry into the BIOIA / WUOM ecosystem through a particular piece, within a specific territory and moment.

It is not:

- a position;
- an administrative unit;
- a superior category;
- a command center;
- an isolated piece;
- a fixed classification.

It is a **position of reading and relationship**.

### 4.1. Provisional formula

```text
🐝📍🧩
=
entry piece
+ place
+ moment
+ signals
+ relationships
+ vulnerability
+ orientation
+ return
```

### 4.2. The piece

The piece is the point from which someone enters or decides to work:

```text
water · soil · biodiversity · agriculture · temperature
community · energy · connectivity · data · eagle · bee
```

Each piece is a point of entry, not a closed compartment.

### 4.3. The situation

The same piece changes according to:

- the territory;
- the moment;
- the pressures;
- the relationships present;
- vulnerability;
- response capacity;
- the effects of its possible loss.

All pieces therefore possess relational value, but they do not present the same urgency at every moment.

> **There are no superior pieces. There are pieces whose situation requires greater attention.**

---

## 5. Equal relational value, different situated vulnerability

BIOIA / WUOM does not establish a fixed hierarchy of pieces.

There is no piece that is always more important than the others.

This does not mean, however, that all require identical attention at all times.

Situated reading distinguishes among:

```text
equal relational value
+
different situation
+
different vulnerability
+
different urgency
```

A piece may acquire greater temporary relevance because:

- it is subject to immediate pressure;
- it connects many other pieces;
- it contains a signal that has not yet been read;
- its deterioration may trigger cascading effects;
- its loss may be irreversible;
- it makes it possible to orient a useful return to the territory.

Relevance is not superiority. It is a dynamic condition.

---

## 6. From node to return

The node is not complete when a piece has been located or described.

The reading must ask what returns to the territory.

Return may take different forms:

- an orientation;
- a warning;
- a synthesis;
- a well-formulated question;
- a map;
- a document;
- an image;
- a protocol;
- a public signal;
- a stewardship decision;
- a minimal action;
- a decision not to intervene;
- feedback to a community;
- a revision of the reading itself.

Return does not necessarily have to be a technical solution.

It may also be a formulation that returns context, meaning, or capacity for orientation.

### 6.1. Central rule

> **Without return, there is extraction.**

When territorial signals are observed, collected, or interpreted without returning understanding, capacity, or usefulness to the territory, the relationship remains incomplete.

Return does not eliminate the risk of extraction by itself, but it introduces an obligation:

> **The reading must justify what it returns, to whom, and with what effects.**

---

## 7. Situated return

A Situated Return is a return linked to:

- a specific piece;
- an identifiable or territorially coherent territory;
- a current need or signal;
- the relationships that sustain that piece;
- a relevant audience or community;
- an appropriate form of return;
- limits of publication, stewardship, and exposure.

Its main question is:

> **What understanding returns to the territory, for whom, and in what form?**

Not every return should be public.

Some returns may remain:

- under stewardship;
- in limited circulation;
- as internal orientation;
- as an observation record;
- as a provisional warning;
- as a hypothesis not yet suitable for publication.

Publication is one form of return, not the only one.

## 8. First practical instance: farmer, network, and soil

The publication showing the farmer facing the global network constitutes a simple instance of situated return.

### 8.1. Elements

```text
🧑‍🌾 territorial experience
+ 📍 Mediterranean agricultural landscape
+ 🧩 soil, water, crops, biodiversity, and connectivity
+ 🌐 contrast with global abstraction
→ 🪧 public formulation
```

### 8.2. Visual formulation

> **Everything is highly connected… except to the soil.**

The image does not reject technology or present the network as an enemy.

It marks a limit:

> Locating a point is not the same as understanding a territory.

The territory cannot be understood only from above.

To understand it, one must read:

- the water;
- the soil;
- the crops;
- the insects;
- the birds;
- the people;
- the relationships that sustain life.

Without that situated reading, the territory is reduced to an abstraction.

### 8.3. Return

The sign:

> **Without return, there is extraction.**

turns a relational reading into a brief public formulation.

It does not solve the problem, but it returns an orientation:

```text
data without situated reading
→ abstraction

abstraction without return
→ extraction
```

The publication thus functions as a situated return of an editorial and pedagogical nature.

## 11. Provisional validation criteria

A reading can be considered situated when it:

1. recognizes the position from which it observes;
2. identifies the specific piece through which it enters;
3. locates the territory and moment;
4. relates the piece to others;
5. distinguishes relational importance from urgency;
6. recognizes vulnerabilities and limits;
7. avoids asserting unverified causalities;
8. defines what return is possible;
9. identifies to whom it returns;
10. maintains stewardship when publication is not appropriate.

---

## 12. Risks to avoid

### 12.1. Turning the piece into a compartment

A situated piece is an entry into the whole, not an isolated unit.

### 12.2. Confusing relevance with hierarchy

Greater urgency does not make a piece superior.

### 12.3. Confusing data with understanding

Locating, measuring, or representing is not the same as understanding the territory.

### 12.4. Turning return into automatic dissemination

Publishing is not always returning.

### 12.5. Speaking on behalf of the piece

The node does not replace the territory, the community, or the living being observed.

### 12.6. Closing too early

The return may be provisional and keep the case open.
