## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the internal process for deciding whether BIOIA / WUOM can participate in a funding call, project, collaboration, consortium, research activity, agreement or institutional relationship without:

- deforming its architecture;
- compromising its custody;
- turning the territory into an extractive source;
- assuming disproportionate obligations;
- creating an unassessed employment or economic relationship;
- putting at risk the personal situation and benefit entitlement of the custodian.

Document 11 makes it possible to recognize a potential institutional intersection.

Document 12 determines whether that intersection can be materialized.

> No opportunity is accepted because of its funding, visibility or prestige.  
> It is accepted only when genuine compatibility exists.

---

## 2. Scope

This protocol applies before:

- submitting an application;
- joining a consortium;
- accepting a formal invitation;
- signing a letter of support or commitment;
- assuming deliverables;
- providing non-public data;
- assigning or licensing materials;
- accepting payments, fees, awards or compensation;
- appearing as a researcher, adviser, collaborator, provider or beneficiary;
- using the property or a territorial case as a pilot space;
- allowing an entity to represent BIOIA / WUOM before third parties.

It does not replace:

- legal advice;
- tax advice;
- a formal consultation with the Spanish National Social Security Institute (INSS);
- a medical assessment;
- review of the specific terms of each funding call.

---

## 3. Principle of Institutional Precaution

Participation remains under observation until all relevant conditions have been resolved.

> Interest is not equivalent to fit.  
> Invitation is not equivalent to compatibility.  
> Funding is not equivalent to return.  
> Recognition is not equivalent to custody.

The absence of sufficient information is not interpreted as acceptance.

It is classified as:

> pending verification

---

## 4. Compatibility Layers

Every opportunity must pass six layers.

### 4.1. Compatibility with BIOIA

It must be verified that the opportunity:

- recognizes the territory as the basis;
- admits situated experience;
- keeps AI in a proportional role;
- permits relational reading;
- includes verifiable territorial return.

### 4.2. Institutional Compatibility

There must be an entity capable of assuming:

- eligibility;
- coordination;
- scientific responsibility;
- administrative responsibility;
- budget;
- contracting;
- data protection;
- reporting and justification;
- obligations towards the funder.

BIOIA does not replace a missing institutional structure.

### 4.3. Personal and Benefit-Entitlement Compatibility

The concrete form of participation of the custodian must be analysed, particularly when it may involve:

- a contract;
- registration as an employee;
- registration as self-employed;
- remuneration;
- invoicing;
- a continuing obligation;
- a timetable;
- subordination;
- executive responsibility;
- periodic production of deliverables;
- any activity that may result in inclusion in a Spanish Social Security scheme.

### 4.4. Custody Compatibility

The following must be guaranteed:

- recognition of authorship;
- traceability of the corpus;
- preservation of the BIOIA / WUOM designation;
- respect for the licence;
- limits of use;
- non-appropriation;
- no relabelling;
- right of withdrawal in the event of breach.

### 4.5. Territorial and Data Compatibility

It must be defined:

- which territory participates;
- what information is collected;
- for what purpose;
- who can gain access;
- how long it is retained;
- which data must remain protected;
- what capacity remains in the territory at the end.

### 4.6. Operational Compatibility

It must be assessed whether the collaboration is proportional to:

- available time;
- real capacity;
- non-continuous rhythms;
- cognitive load;
- public exposure;
- travel;
- meetings;
- deadlines;
- possibility of interruption.

An opportunity may be conceptually compatible and operationally unviable.

## 5. Specific Filter for Absolute Permanent Disability

### 5.1. General Basis

As of the date of this document, Article 198.2 of the consolidated Spanish General Social Security Act establishes that an absolute permanent-disability pension does not prevent the performance of activities, whether paid or unpaid, that are compatible with the person's condition and do not represent a change in working capacity for review purposes.

The same provision establishes that, when the work or activity results in inclusion in a Social Security scheme, the managing entity will suspend payment of the pension for the duration of that activity and resume it when the activity ceases, without prejudice to a possible review of the degree of disability.

Spanish Social Security also indicates that the start of employed or self-employed work subject to registration and contributions must be reported to the National Social Security Institute.

### 5.2. Operational Consequence

The compatibility of a form of participation must not be presumed merely because it:

- is intellectual;
- is carried out from home;
- is digital;
- involves few hours;
- is called collaboration;
- is presented as volunteering;
- involves a small payment;
- describes payment as aid, an award or a licence;
- is informally described by the entity as presenting no risk.

The real legal and economic form prevails over the name used.

### 5.3. Stop Rule

Before accepting an activity that may involve registration, contributions, a contract, invoicing, regular obligations or remuneration linked to personal work, the process stops.

Depending on the case, the following must be obtained:

- a written consultation with the INSS;
- review by a professional specializing in Social Security law;
- tax analysis;
- a written description of functions, dedication, consideration and duration.

> Without written verification, no commitment is assumed.

### 5.4. Protection of Personal Information

Benefit-entitlement and medical information must not be incorporated into public documents, project reports or general communications.

Only the information strictly necessary is communicated to:

- the adviser;
- the responsible entity;
- the INSS;
- the body required to verify compatibility.

## 6. Possible Forms of Relationship

These categories do not automatically determine compatibility. They organize the level of risk and the verification required.

### A. Consultation or Reading of Public Materials

Examples:

- an entity learns about BIOIA;
- cites public documents;
- studies its architecture;
- requests an exploratory conversation without commitment.

**Initial level:** low.  
**Condition:** respect for the licence, attribution and non-appropriation.

### B. Occasional Non-Contractual Exchange

Examples:

- an occasional conversation;
- response to an enquiry;
- a limited contribution;
- one-off participation without deliverables or continuity.

**Initial level:** low or medium.  
**Condition:** state in writing that there is no continuing obligation, employment relationship or assignment of the corpus.

### C. Formal Unpaid Collaboration

Examples:

- letter of support;
- committee participation;
- contribution to a project report;
- nominal presence in a project;
- use of the territory as a case.

**Initial level:** medium or high.  
**Condition:** verify obligations, dedication, responsibility, data, exposure and compatibility with benefit entitlement.

The absence of remuneration does not by itself eliminate risks.

### D. Paid Participation

Examples:

- contract;
- fees;
- consultancy;
- invoicing;
- grant or scholarship;
- payment for deliverables;
- paid licence;
- periodic remuneration;
- registration as an employee or self-employed person.

**Initial level:** high.  
**Condition:** mandatory stop and prior legal, benefit-entitlement and tax verification.

### E. Recognition Without Personal Participation

Examples:

- an institution uses public materials under licence;
- cites BIOIA;
- recognizes its authorship;
- incorporates the framework without requiring personal work;
- maintains contact only on attribution or custody matters.

**Initial level:** variable, generally lower.  
**Condition:** verify that there is no appropriation, improper representation or concealed obligation.

### F. Intermediary Entity

The existence of an association, foundation, university or collaborating entity does not automatically resolve personal compatibility.

It must be verified:

- who materially performs the work;
- who receives the funds;
- which obligations fall on the custodian;
- whether direct or indirect consideration exists.

> Interposing an entity does not turn an incompatible activity into a compatible one.

---

## 7. Custody Verification

Before non-public materials are provided, there must be a written agreement determining:

1. Which documents are shared.
2. For what purpose.
3. For how long.
4. Who can gain access.
5. What can be published.
6. How attribution is made.
7. Which transformations are permitted.
8. Which uses are excluded.
9. How territorial and personal data are protected.
10. What happens when the relationship ends.
11. How materials are withdrawn.
12. How breaches are resolved.

The following are not provided:

- keys;
- complete internal archives;
- sensitive data;
- precise ecological locations;
- personal information;
- protected versions of the corpus;

without a delimited purpose and sufficient safeguards.

---

## 8. Non-Appropriation Conditions

The opportunity is incompatible when it requires or produces:

- exclusive assignment of the corpus;
- transfer of authorship;
- a change of name;
- absorption of BIOIA into an institutional brand;
- publication without traceability;
- presentation of the architecture as a creation of the consortium;
- blocking the custodian's access to their own materials;
- patents or exclusive rights over pre-existing elements;
- unauthorized commercial use;
- inability to withdraw from the collaboration.

New results must clearly distinguish:

> pre-existing corpus  
> + contribution made during the project  
> + shared result

---

## 9. Territorial Return

Every participation must identify a concrete return.

It may consist of:

- accessible knowledge;
- useful documentation;
- improved observation capacity;
- ecological protection;
- community strengthening;
- access to tools;
- training;
- institutional improvement;
- preservation of memory;
- decision-making capacity.

The following are not sufficient return by themselves:

- visibility;
- mentions;
- logos;
- dissemination;
- metrics;
- academic publications;
- data capture;
- non-verifiable future promises.

> The territory does not participate only to validate an external project.

---

## 10. Warning Signals

The assessment stops when any of the following signals appear:

- urgency to sign;
- undefined functions;
- verbal promises without documentation;
- pressure to provide the complete corpus;
- incompatibility between environmental discourse and extractive practice;
- remuneration without a clear legal form;
- open-ended or unlimited deliverables;
- requirement for continuing availability;
- minimization of benefit-entitlement risk;
- request for unnecessary sensitive data;
- use of the territory without return;
- exclusivity;
- appropriation of the name or authorship;
- inability to withdraw;
- assertion that compatibility “will be resolved later”.

> What cannot be explained beforehand must not be accepted first.

## 11. Assessment Procedure

### Phase 1. Opportunity Record

Record:

- entity;
- funding call or project;
- date;
- contact;
- declared purpose;
- deadline;
- proposed function;
- funding;
- available documentation.

### Phase 2. Fit with Document 11

Determine whether a real intersection with BIOIA exists.

Result:

- fits;
- partially fits;
- does not fit;
- insufficient information.

### Phase 3. Material Description of Participation

The entity must state in writing:

- function;
- tasks;
- dedication;
- duration;
- meetings;
- travel;
- deliverables;
- responsibility;
- remuneration;
- ownership of results;
- use of data;
- exit conditions.

### Phase 4. Personal and Benefit-Entitlement Verification

Determine whether there may be:

- an employment relationship;
- self-employed activity;
- registration or contributions;
- remuneration;
- review of benefit entitlement;
- mandatory notification to the INSS.

Where doubt exists, the status remains:

> pending external verification

### Phase 5. Custody and Return Verification

Apply Documents 09, 10, 11 and this protocol.

### Phase 6. Decision

The decision must be recorded and dated.

An opportunity is not accepted merely because the deadline is approaching.

## 12. Decision Matrix

| Dimension | Compatible | Conditional | Incompatible |
|---|---|---|---|
| BIOIA architecture | Respects territory, experience, proportional AI and return | Requires delimitation | Requires deformation of BIOIA |
| Entity | Assumes responsibilities | Safeguards must be specified | Transfers inappropriate responsibilities |
| Absolute permanent disability and personal situation | No problematic activity appears, or written verification exists | Requires specialist consultation | Involves an unacceptable risk |
| Function | Limited and clear | Partially defined | Open-ended, continuous or disproportionate |
| Remuneration | Verified and compatible | Pending analysis | Acceptance required without verification |
| Custody | Authorship and limits guaranteed | Requires agreement | Assignment, absorption or relabelling |
| Data | Purpose and access delimited | Safeguards are incomplete | Extraction or exposure of sensitive information |
| Return | Concrete and verifiable | Still incomplete | Absent or merely reputational |
| Exit | Possible and regulated | Must be incorporated | No real right of withdrawal |

---

## 13. Final Classification

### 13.1. Compatible

All essential conditions are resolved and documented.

### 13.2. Compatible with Conditions

Fit exists, but participation depends on explicit prior conditions.

It does not begin until they have been fulfilled.

### 13.3. Pending Verification

Legal, benefit-entitlement, economic, operational or custody information is missing.

No commitment exists.

### 13.4. Incompatible

The opportunity requires deformation, extraction, appropriation, an unacceptable benefit-entitlement risk or absence of return.

It is archived with the reason recorded.

## 16. Final Criterion

BIOIA can relate to institutions without becoming an employment, commercial or extractive structure incompatible with its nature and with the situation of its custodian.

Expansion of scope does not justify:

- putting personal protection at risk;
- accepting unsustainable obligations;
- surrendering the corpus;
- weakening territorial return.

> An opportunity exists only when it can be accepted without its cost destroying what it seeks to expand.
