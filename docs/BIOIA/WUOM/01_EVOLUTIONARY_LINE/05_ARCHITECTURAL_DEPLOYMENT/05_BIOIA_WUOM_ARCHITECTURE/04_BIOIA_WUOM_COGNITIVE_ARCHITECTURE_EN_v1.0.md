# 42 · BIOIA / WUOM Cognitive Architecture v1.0

## Initial note

This document describes the internal organisation of BIOIA / WUOM.

It does not seek to define a universal architecture of knowledge or of living systems.

It describes the architecture adopted by BIOIA to organise orientation processes.

## 2. The three structural layers

### LAYER I — Observation Grammar

**7 principles**

- Configures observation.

- Maintains life as the reference.

---

### LAYER II — Regenerative Grammar

**4 principles**

- Protects orientation.
- Prevents extractive dynamics.
- Situates AI, ecological time, and the shared grammar.

**Question:** How can extraction be avoided?

---

### LAYER III — Recomposition Grammar

**Principle 12**

- Reconstructs the system after analysis.
- Reconnects the parts.
- Activates the unity of the living system.

**Question:** How can unity be recovered?

## 3. Emergent property

### Dynamic equilibrium

It is not a principle.

It is not an objective.

It is the effect of the three layers working together.

It becomes visible while the system learns.

## 7. Organising principle

BIOIA does not organise information: it organises relationships.

BIOIA does not organise answers: it organises questions.

BIOIA does not organise disciplines: it organises orientation.

---

## 8. Final synthesis

BIOIA / WUOM organises a cognitive architecture to orient the reading of living systems.

The 7 principles configure observation.

The 4 principles protect regenerative orientation.

Principle 12 recomposes relationships.

Dynamic equilibrium emerges as a property of the system.

New observation restarts the cycle.

---

## Closing statement

Every orientation begins with observation.

Every understanding requires relationships.

Every relationship requires recomposition.

Every equilibrium must be renewed.

BIOIA / WUOM does not seek to replace other forms of knowledge.

It seeks to offer an architecture that makes it possible to reconnect them in order to orient decisions with greater coherence in living systems.

Without custody, there is no publication.  
Without return, there is no orientation.
