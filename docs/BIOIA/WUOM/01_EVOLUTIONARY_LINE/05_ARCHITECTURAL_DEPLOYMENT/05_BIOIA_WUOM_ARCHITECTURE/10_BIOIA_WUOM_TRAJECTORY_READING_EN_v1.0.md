# 10 · BIOIA / WUOM Trajectory Reading v1.0

**BIOIA / WUOM v1.0**  
**Operative Architecture**  
**Status:** Stable

---

## Purpose

To incorporate the temporal dimension as a structural reading criterion.

BIOIA does not interpret only isolated states or events. Its unit of analysis is the trajectory that emerges when signals are observed continuously over time.

---

## Principle

> **An isolated signal informs. A trajectory orients.**

A photograph shows an instant.

A trajectory shows a direction.

Orientation arises from understanding how relationships evolve over time.

---

## Temporal unit

BIOIA works with verifiable sequences.

It does not seek to anticipate the future through prediction.

It seeks to understand the direction shown by observed signals.

### Conceptual example

```text
2020 → 2021 → 2022 → 2023 → 2024 → 2025 → 2026
```

Each year adds information.

The complete trajectory makes it possible to interpret relationships that remain hidden when only a single instant is observed.

---

## Territorial Film v1

**Territorial Film v1** constitutes the first operative exercise in trajectory reading within BIOIA.

Its function is not to prove a theory.

Its function is to make it possible to observe the continuity of territorial signals.

Territorial return continuously validates or corrects the reading.

---

The **Time Tree** provides the temporal dimension.

Time and trajectory form an operative unit.

Trajectory reading can be carried out across multiple scales:

- Hours
- Days

Each process has its own rhythm.

Not all trajectories evolve at the same speed.

## Validation criterion

BIOIA does not validate an orientation because a prediction was fulfilled.

It validates it through continuous territorial return.

The observed trajectory confirms, corrects, or modifies the orientation.

## Central idea

> **BIOIA does not observe only what is happening. It observes the direction in which the territory is evolving.**

Orientation does not arise from the instant.

It arises from the trajectory.

---

## Scale of observation and intervention

BIOIA distinguishes between the scale of reading and the scale of action.

Observation may extend across multiple territories in order to identify common patterns.

Intervention always takes place in a specific territory.

```text
🌍 Macro view
   ↓
🧩 Relate trajectories
   ↓
🧭 Orient
   ↓
📍 Micro action
   ↓
🔄 Territorial return
   ↓
🌍 New macro reading
```

Orientation connects both scales.

A broad view makes it possible to understand relationships that are not visible from a single location.

Situated action makes it possible to adapt decisions to the specific characteristics of each territory.

---

## Operative principle

> **The view may be global.**  
> **Orientation must integrate relationships.**  
> **Action is always situated.**

---

## Methodological note

The existence of common patterns across different territories does not imply that there is a single solution.

BIOIA uses the macro view to learn from multiple experiences and micro action to adapt that knowledge to each context.

Territorial return feeds the global reading again, closing a continuous learning cycle.
