**Status:** Stable v1.0

## Purpose

This document integrates the BIOIA / WUOM operative architecture into a single map of functioning.

It does not add new interfaces or modify existing ones.

Its function is to show how the methodological laws, the interfaces, and the operative flow form a single system of territorial reading.

---

## Current state of the system

BIOIA / WUOM constitutes an operative system of territorial reading based on the integration of:

- three methodological laws;
- ten specialised interfaces;
- one common flow of interpretation;
- permanent return to the territory.

The system does not seek to replace human experience or automate decisions.

Its purpose is to improve orientation before action.

---

## Methodological laws

Every reading carried out through BIOIA / WUOM is governed by three common laws.

### 1. Law of Territorial Return

Every interpretation must return to the territory for contrast.

Without return, there is no validation.

### 2. Law of Methodological Neutrality

BIOIA / WUOM does not favour ideologies, particular interests, or personal preferences.

The tool organises the reading.

It does not determine the decision.

### 3. Law of Operative Uncertainty

Every reading incorporates uncertainty.

Uncertainty does not invalidate orientation.

It forms part of the territory itself.

---

## Stabilised interfaces

BIOIA / WUOM currently integrates ten operative interfaces:

1. Genetics
2. Communication
3. Water
4. Biodiversity
5. Community
6. Time
7. Temperature
8. Connectivity
9. Energy
10. Soil

Each observes a specific aspect.

All use the same methodology.

---

## Common operative flow

All interfaces follow the same sequence:

```text
Observe
   ↓
Interpret
   ↓
Relate
   ↓
Orient
   ↓
Return to the territory
```

Orientation appears before action.

Action is verified through return.

---

## Relationship between interfaces

The interfaces do not function in isolation.

Each contributes a partial reading of the territory.

Their integration makes it possible to understand relationships that do not appear when each element is analysed separately.

The system organises relationships.

It does not accumulate information.

---

## Integrated territorial reading

BIOIA / WUOM understands the territory as an organism composed of multiple simultaneous processes.

Water, soil, biodiversity, energy, community, temperature, time, and connectivity evolve together.

Changing one condition can affect the whole.

For this reason, the unit of reading is territorial.

---

## Situated orientation

BIOIA / WUOM does not generate universal answers.

Each orientation depends on the observed context.

The methodology remains stable.

The application changes according to the territory, the moment, and the existing conditions.

---

## Territorial return

Every orientation must return to the territory in order to verify its usefulness.

Return makes it possible to:

- confirm;
- correct;
- learn;
- update the reading.

Without return, the system loses its capacity to learn.

---

## Synthesis

BIOIA / WUOM is not a collection of independent interfaces.

It is an operative architecture in which laws, interfaces, and methodology work together to improve the reading of the territory before any decision.

Its purpose is not to decide on behalf of people.

Its purpose is to increase the capacity to understand relationships, reduce disorientation, and support situated decisions that can be verified through territorial return.
