# Stage Closure · BIOIA Architecture

During this phase, BIOIA stopped growing through the accumulation of elements and began to grow through the integration of relationships.

The architecture acquired a recognisable centre, an internal sequence, and an origin narrative.

The new pieces did not replace the previous ones; they reorganised their meaning within a coherent whole.

From this point onward, development no longer depends primarily on incorporating concepts. It depends on verification through cases, time, and return.

The future evolution of BIOIA will consist less in expanding the architecture than in testing, correcting, and enriching its orientation capacities within real systems.
