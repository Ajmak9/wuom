## Purpose

This document begins the audit of the **BIOIA / WUOM Tree nursery**.

Its function is not to create new interfaces.

Its function is to review the existing Trees, distinguish which have already been integrated, which remain candidates, and which require further observation.

---

## Methodological framework

The audit applies the document:

> **06 · Selection Criteria for New BIOIA / WUOM Interfaces v1.0**

Every review must consider:

- repeated territorial evidence;
- an organising centre of its own;
- no overlap;
- verifiable return;
- real operative usefulness;
- systemic necessity.

## Preliminary audit of strong candidates

### 1. Fire Tree

**Status:** Active candidate.

**Territorial signal:**  
Wildfires, extreme heat, dryness, rural abandonment, plant fuel, wind, forest management, and territorial risk.

**Possible organising centre:**  
The organisation of fire as a threshold of territorial transformation.

**Overlap:**  
Temperature, Soil, Biodiversity, Energy, Community, and Wind.

**Preliminary reading:**  
Strong candidate. It cannot be reduced to temperature. It may justify an organ of its own if its territorial return and organising centre are confirmed.

### 2. Wind Tree

**Status:** Active candidate.

**Territorial signal:**  
Evaporation, dispersion, fire, wind energy, pollination, erosion, thermal comfort, and atmospheric movement.

**Possible organising centre:**  
The organisation of atmospheric movement as a territorial force.

**Overlap:**  
Energy, Temperature, Water, Fire, Connectivity.

**Preliminary reading:**  
Relevant candidate. It must be assessed whether it operates as an interface of its own or as a transversal relationship between other interfaces.

### 3. Fog Tree

**Status:** Advanced observation.

**Territorial signal:**  
Humidity, visibility, temperature, condensation, atmosphere, orientation, and microclimate.

**Possible organising centre:**  
The organisation of visibility and atmospheric humidity under specific territorial conditions.

**Overlap:**  
Water, Temperature, Time, Communication, Orientation.

**Preliminary reading:**  
Interesting candidate, but probably specific. It may remain under observation before being considered an interface.

### 4. Territory Tree

**Status:** Structural candidate.

**Territorial signal:**  
The entire BIOIA / WUOM system is articulated around the territory.

**Possible organising centre:**  
The organisation of territory as a common field of reading.

**Overlap:**  
Very high.

**Preliminary reading:**  
It probably should not become an interface, because Territory functions as the general framework of the system. There is a high risk of absorbing all organs.

### 5. Living Tree

**Status:** Strong conceptual candidate.

**Territorial signal:**  
Life, organisms, metabolism, continuity, coexistence, habitability.

**Possible organising centre:**  
The organisation of the living as a condition of territorial continuity.

**Overlap:**  
Biodiversity, Genetics, Soil, Community.

**Preliminary reading:**  
Conceptually powerful, but it must avoid duplicating Biodiversity or Genetics. It may be a transversal framework rather than an interface.

### 6. Climate Tree

**Status:** Transversal candidate.

**Territorial signal:**  
Temperature, rain, drought, wind, humidity, fire, seasonality, extreme events.

**Possible organising centre:**  
The organisation of climatic conditions as a dynamic framework of the territory.

**Overlap:**  
Very high with Temperature, Water, Time, Wind, Fire, and Energy.

**Preliminary reading:**  
It may be too broad for an interface. It is probably preferable to keep it as a transversal framework or synthesis layer.

## Conclusion

The nursery shows a high richness of possible lines, but it does not yet require the immediate opening of a new interface.

The strongest candidates for future review are:

1. Fire.
2. Wind.
3. Climate.
4. Living.
5. Fog.

However, none should be incorporated automatically into the first operative circle.

Each requires a complete application of Document 06 before becoming a pre-interface.

---

## Nursery update rule

Every new Tree that appears in the future must follow this process:

```text
New Tree
   ↓
Entry into nursery
   ↓
Application of Document 06
   ↓
Assigned status
   ↓
Observation / Integration / Pre-interface / Discard
```
