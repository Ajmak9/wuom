# 01 · Definition and Scope of the BIOIA / WUOM Master Plan

## Status

**Constitutive document**  
**Version:** Stable

---

## Nature of the Master Plan

The Master Plan is the canonical representation of the complete BIOIA / WUOM architecture.

It is not an infographic, a conceptual map, or an operative diagram.

It is the stable structure that organises the system's components and makes it possible to situate any piece within the whole.

The Master Plan represents stable relationships, not temporary states.

---

## Function

The Master Plan fulfils one principal function:

> **To situate each element within BIOIA / WUOM.**

It does not detail.  
It does not narrate.  
It does not develop.  
It does not interpret.

It orients.

---

## Central question

> **Where is this piece located within the system?**

---

## Scope

The Master Plan represents:

- the BIOIA / WUOM Core;
- the Cognitive Architecture;
- the Law of Territorial Return;
- the Principles;
- the Tree Family;
- the Interfaces;
- the Applications;
- the structural relationships between these elements;
- and the flows of reading, orientation, and return.

---

## Limits

The Master Plan does not represent:

- detailed operative content;
- specific use cases;
- complete methodologies;
- technical implementations;
- graphic styles;
- temporary versions of the system;
- or unconsolidated experimental elements.

It includes only stable, documented, and structurally necessary components.

---

## Relationship with the BIOIA / WUOM Core

The Core defines the system's intention:

> **Reading · Orientation · Return**

The Master Plan represents the system's organised form.

The Core defines the logic.  
The Master Plan defines the form.

---

## Relationship with the Cognitive Architecture

The Cognitive Architecture describes the system's internal organisation:

- Observation Grammar;
- Regenerative Grammar;
- Recomposition Grammar;
- Dynamic State of Balance;
- Continuous Cognitive Cycle.

The Master Plan does not replace the Cognitive Architecture.

It situates it within the whole.

---

## Relationship with the Law of Territorial Return

The Law of Territorial Return is the system's structural condition.

The Master Plan must show that every flow begins and ends in the territory.

Territorial return is not an addition.

It is the system's closure condition.

---

## Relationship with the Principles

The Principles are orientation rules.

The Master Plan does not develop them.

It situates them as a bridge between the Cognitive Architecture, the Tree Family, and the Interfaces.

---

## Relationship with the Tree Family

The Trees are perspectives for reading the living system.

The Master Plan must show:

- their relationship with the Principles;
- their connection with the Interfaces;
- their function as reading organs;
- and their capacity to grow without altering the architecture.

---

## Relationship with the Interfaces

The Interfaces are the system's operative gateways.

The Master Plan must show:

- their dependence on the Core;
- their relationship with the Trees;
- their function as a bridge towards Applications;
- and their capacity to enable situated decisions.

---

## Relationship with the Applications

Applications are situated uses of the system.

The Master Plan does not describe them in detail.

It only shows their place within the flow:

```text
Territory → Reading → Orientation → Return → Territory
```

---

## Traceability

Every element of the Master Plan must be traceable to a real system document.

Examples:

- Core → `01_BIOIA_WUOM_CORE`
- Architecture → Cognitive Architecture
- Law → Law of Territorial Return
- Principles → Foundational documents
- Trees → Tree Family
- Interfaces → Interface documents
- Applications → Situated cases and developments

The Master Plan is an architectural index.

It does not contain all the information.

It points to it.

---

## Status within the system

The Master Plan is not:

- the Core;
- a Principle;
- a Tree;
- an Interface;
- or an Application.

It is the system's representational infrastructure.

---

## Purpose

The Master Plan makes it possible to:

- teach the system;
- transmit it;
- expand it;
- maintain it;
- audit it;
- and orient its evolution.

Its function is to preserve the structural coherence of BIOIA / WUOM while the system evolves.

---

## Closing statement

The Master Plan does not explain BIOIA / WUOM.

It situates it.

Its value does not lie in showing everything.

It lies in making it possible to understand where each piece belongs within the system.

> **Without custody, there is no publication.**  
> **Without return, there is no orientation.**
