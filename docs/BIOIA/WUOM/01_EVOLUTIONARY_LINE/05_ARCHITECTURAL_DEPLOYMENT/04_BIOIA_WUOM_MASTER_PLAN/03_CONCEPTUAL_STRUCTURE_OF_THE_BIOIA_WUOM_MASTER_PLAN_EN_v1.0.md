# 03 · Conceptual Structure of the BIOIA / WUOM Master Plan

## Status

**Constitutive document**  
**Version:** Stable

---

## Purpose

This document defines the conceptual organisation of the BIOIA / WUOM Master Plan.

It does not represent operative content.

It does not describe specific implementations.

It defines the stable architecture that organises all system components and the relationships between them.

---

# Structural components

## 1. BIOIA / WUOM Core

**Reading · Orientation · Return**

### Structural role

Organises the meaning of the system and articulates its fundamental movement.

### Relationships

- Organises the Cognitive Architecture.
- Orients the overall meaning of the system.
- Connects reading and return.
- Maintains continuity between territory and orientation.

---

## 2. Cognitive Architecture

**Observation Grammar · Regenerative Grammar · Recomposition Grammar**

### Structural role

Organises the internal structure of the system.

Distributes the cognitive capacities of BIOIA / WUOM.

### Relationships

- Organises the Principles.
- Organises the Tree Family.
- Organises the Interfaces.

The Architecture does not contain.

The Architecture structures.

---

## 3. Law of Territorial Return

### Structural role

Conditions the entire system transversally.

Ensures that every orientation concludes through territorial return.

### Relationships

- Conditions every level.
- Verifies Applications and decisions.
- Maintains closure of the cycle.

The Law does not constitute a hierarchical level.

It is a transversal structural condition.

---

## 4. BIOIA / WUOM Principles

### Structural role

Act as orientation rules.

Connect the Cognitive Architecture with the Tree Family and the Interfaces.

### Relationships

- Derive from the Cognitive Architecture.
- Orient the Trees.
- Orient the Interfaces.

The Principles do not contain.

The Principles orient.

---

## 5. Tree Family

### Structural role

Constitutes the perspectives for reading the living system.

### Relationships

- Read the territory.
- Feed the Interfaces.
- Derive from the Principles.

The Trees do not belong to the Principles.

They are oriented through them.

---

## 6. BIOIA / WUOM Interfaces

### Structural role

Constitute the system's operative gateways.

### Relationships

- Connect Trees and Applications.
- Enable situated decisions.
- Return capacity to the territory.

The Interfaces do not organise.

The Interfaces connect.

---

## 7. Applications

### Structural role

Materialise orientation in concrete situations.

### Relationships

- Materialise orientation.
- Verify in the territory.
- Return to the cycle.

Applications do not explain.

Applications execute.

---

# Emergent property

## System legibility

When all components maintain their structural relationships, the Master Plan makes it possible to understand the organisation of BIOIA / WUOM without first reviewing all of its documentation.

This property is not designed.

It emerges from coherence between the components and their relationships.

---

# Status within BIOIA / WUOM

The Master Plan belongs to the set of constitutive documents together with:

- the BIOIA / WUOM Core;
- the Cognitive Architecture;
- the Law of Territorial Return.

Everything else — Principles, Trees, Interfaces, and Applications — constitutes an organised expression of this core.

The Master Plan is not an Application.

It is not an Interface.

It is not an infographic.

It is the system's representational infrastructure.

---

# Closing statement

The Master Plan represents stable relationships, not temporary states.

Its function is to preserve the structural coherence of BIOIA / WUOM while the system evolves.

It does not determine how the system must grow.

It ensures that any growth can find its place without losing identity.

> **Without custody, there is no publication.**  
> **Without return, there is no orientation.**
