## Principle 013 · Territorial Unit of Orientation

> **The unit of intervention may be local.  
> The unit of reading must be the territory that sustains that intervention.**

---

## Formulation

Situated orientation cannot be limited solely to the place where action is taken.

An intervention may be carried out on a farm, in a neighborhood, a city, a watershed, an infrastructure, or a specific community.

But its reading must incorporate the territory that sustains it:

- soil,
- water,
- biodiversity,
- energy,
- temperature,
- connectivity,
- community,
- time,
- natural ecosystems,
- rural territories,
- agricultural systems,
- technical and social networks.

---

## Meaning of the Principle

A city is not an isolated system.

A rural area is not a residual component of the urban system.

A natural ecosystem is not an external backdrop.

All of them form part of the same territorial organization.

Therefore, an adaptation or mitigation strategy cannot be limited to creating green and blue spaces within cities while the territory that sustains them continues to lose ecological, hydrological, thermal, and biological capacity.

---

## Operational Criterion

> **The city is not a bubble.**  
> **The network is not a bubble.**  
> **Every human infrastructure depends on a living territorial foundation.**

The intervention may begin at a single point.

The reading must open onto the system that allows that point to exist.

---

## BIOIA/WUOM Application

BIOIA/WUOM does not orient isolated elements.

It orients territorial relationships.

Therefore, in response to a signal such as rising temperatures, the response cannot be reduced to:

```text
🌇 + 🌳 + 💧

🌍 + 🌱 + 💧 + 🌿 + ⚡ + 📡 + 👥 + ⌛
```

In other words:

urban adaptation, yes;

but integrated within a broader territorial strategy.

---

## Related Principle

It is not enough to adapt the places where we live.

We must also preserve the processes that allow those places to remain habitable.

---

## Closing

The unit of intervention may be partial.

The unit of reading must be territorial.

Without territorial reading, there is no situated orientation.
