Status: Active  
Type: Cross-cutting methodological law  
Version: v1.0

Name

Law of Operational Uncertainty

Formulation

Every territorial reading contains a degree of uncertainty.

BIOIA/WUOM does not eliminate uncertainty.

It makes it visible, explicit, and manageable.

Principle

The absence of absolute certainty does not prevent orientation.

It prevents claiming more than the evidence allows.

Purpose

To prevent orientation from becoming dogma.

To keep open the possibility of correcting a reading when new signals appear.

Method

Every orientation must indicate:

- which signals support it;
- which relationships have been observed;
- what degree of uncertainty remains;
- what return would allow it to be confirmed or revised.

Return

Return does not prove that a reading was perfect.

It reduces the remaining uncertainty.

Each new return improves the capacity for orientation.

Operational criterion

Do not decide with excessive confidence.

Do not become paralyzed by excessive uncertainty.

Orient using the best available evidence.

Relationship with other laws

Law of Territorial Return

→ verifies orientations.

Law of Methodological Neutrality

→ protects the impartiality of the reading.

Law of Operational Uncertainty

→ recognizes the limits of the available knowledge.

The three operate jointly.

Synthesis

Uncertainty is not a system error.

It is a permanent condition of territorial reading.

BIOIA/WUOM does not seek to eliminate it. It seeks to make it legible, explicit, and progressively reducible through territorial return.
