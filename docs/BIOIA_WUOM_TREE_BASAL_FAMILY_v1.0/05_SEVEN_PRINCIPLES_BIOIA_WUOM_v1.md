## Seven Principles for Reading Living Systems

**Version:** 1.0  
**Language:** English  
**Project:** BIOIA / WUOM — Basal Family

---

# Purpose

The Seven Principles provide the common orientation shared by every BIOIA / WUOM Tree.

They are not a methodology.

They are a way of reading living systems before making decisions.

Together they define the ethical and operational foundation of the BIOIA approach.

---

# Principle 1 — Observe before acting

Every decision begins with careful observation.

Signals appear before conclusions.

Observation precedes interpretation.

---

# Principle 2 — Read relationships

Living systems cannot be understood by isolated elements.

Meaning emerges from relationships.

The system is more than the sum of its parts.

---

# Principle 3 — Territory comes first

Every observation belongs to a specific place.

Context gives meaning.

Reality is always situated.

The network emerges from the territory—not the other way around.

---

# Principle 4 — Minimum necessary intervention

Intervention should never replace natural processes unnecessarily.

Understand first.

Act only when needed.

Support before replacing.

---

# Principle 5 — Return to the territory

Knowledge has value only if it returns to reality.

Observation generates learning.

Learning improves orientation.

Orientation produces better decisions.

The territory verifies the return.

---

# Principle 6 — Continuous learning

Every observation improves the next one.

BIOIA remains open to correction.

Understanding evolves through experience, evidence and dialogue.

Learning never ends.

---

# Principle 7 — Life at the center

Life is the reference.

Technology is a tool.

Data are tools.

Models are tools.

The continuity of living systems is the purpose.

---

# BIOIA / WUOM

BIOIA integrates the readings.

WUOM organizes orientation.

The territory verifies the return.

Together they transform observation into situated understanding.

---

# Core Statement

Observe carefully.

Understand relationships.

Start from the territory.

Intervene with humility.

Return knowledge to reality.

Continue learning.

Keep life at the center.

---

## Final Principle

A network has value only when it grows from living relationships rooted in a territory.

Without territory there is no orientation.

Without return there is no learning.
