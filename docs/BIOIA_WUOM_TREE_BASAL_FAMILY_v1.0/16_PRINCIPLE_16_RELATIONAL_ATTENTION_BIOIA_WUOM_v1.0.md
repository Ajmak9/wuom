# 🌳 Principle 16 · Relational Attention

## Central Idea

The ecosystemic perspective develops relational attention.

## Development

Fragmented attention concentrates observation on isolated events.

Relational attention seeks to understand the relationships that connect those events within the same system.

A signal may attract attention.

Orientation emerges when the relationships among signals begin to become visible.

BIOIA does not replace the observation of events.

It proposes integrating them into a relational reading of the territory.
