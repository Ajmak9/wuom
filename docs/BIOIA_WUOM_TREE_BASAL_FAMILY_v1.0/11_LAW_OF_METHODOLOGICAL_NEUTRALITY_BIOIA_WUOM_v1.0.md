## Status

**Version:** v1.0  
**Type:** Cross-cutting methodological law  
**Status:** Active

---

# Name

**Law of Methodological Neutrality**

---

# Formulation

BIOIA/WUOM does not organize the reading of the territory according to personal preferences, ideological positions, or partisan interests.

Its function is to organize a verifiable reading of the conditions of coexistence and territorial continuity.

---

# Purpose

To clearly separate:

- the author;
- the tool;
- human decisions.

Each occupies a distinct function.

---

# The author

Personal experience may give rise to questions.

It may motivate observations.

It may initiate an investigation.

But it does not determine the system’s conclusions.

---

# The tool

BIOIA/WUOM does not belong to:

- the left;
- the center;
- the right;
- an ideological current;
- a political doctrine.

Its permanent reference is the territory.

---

# The decision

Decisions belong to:

- people;
- communities;
- institutions;
- public administrations;
- organizations.

BIOIA/WUOM does not replace deliberation.

It contributes a reading that can help orient it.

---

# Method

Every orientation must be capable of being:

- observed;
- related;
- contrasted;
- verified through territorial return.

When the evidence contradicts a prior interpretation, the interpretation must be revised.

Not the territory.

---

# Operational criterion

The author’s preferences do not constitute evidence.

Territorial evidence takes priority over preferences.

---

# Scope

This law applies to all BIOIA/WUOM interfaces.

It includes:

- Soil;
- Water;
- Temperature;
- Biodiversity;
- Community;
- Energy;
- Connectivity;
- Time;
- and any future development.

---

# Relationship with the Law of Territorial Return

The Law of Territorial Return verifies orientations.

Methodological Neutrality protects the reading process.

Both operate jointly.

---

# Synthesis

> **Experience may initiate the question.**

> **The territory verifies the answer.**

> **BIOIA/WUOM orients through reading, not through preferences, ideologies, or partisan positions.**
