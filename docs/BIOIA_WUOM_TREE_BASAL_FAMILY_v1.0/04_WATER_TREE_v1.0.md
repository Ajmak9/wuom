# 04_WATER_TREE_v1.0
## Reading Water as a Living System

**Version:** 1.0  
**Language:** English  
**Project:** BIOIA / WUOM — Basal Tree Family

---

# Core idea

The Water Tree reads water as a living system of relationships.

Water is not only a resource.
It is circulation, storage, memory, exchange and continuity.

Understanding water requires reading how it moves, where it remains, what it connects and how it sustains the territory.

BIOIA does not reduce water to volume.
It reads water as a structural component of living territorial systems.

---

# What the Water Tree reads

The Water Tree observes multiple dimensions simultaneously.

- Precipitation
- Surface runoff
- Infiltration
- Soil moisture
- Groundwater
- Springs
- Rivers
- Wetlands
- Retention areas
- Water quality
- Connectivity between aquatic systems
- Seasonal variability

The objective is not only to measure water.

The objective is to understand its role inside the territorial system.

---

# Water as a connector

Water links multiple components of the territory.

It connects:

- Soil
- Vegetation
- Biodiversity
- Agriculture
- Climate
- Human settlements
- Ecosystem processes

Changes in water frequently appear before visible changes in the landscape.

Water is often an early signal of territorial transformation.

---

# Observable signals

Examples include:

- Changes in river flow
- Drying springs
- Flood frequency
- Soil saturation
- Water retention capacity
- Surface water connectivity
- Wetland condition
- Water quality
- Sediment transport
- Seasonal anomalies

These observations become meaningful only when interpreted together.
