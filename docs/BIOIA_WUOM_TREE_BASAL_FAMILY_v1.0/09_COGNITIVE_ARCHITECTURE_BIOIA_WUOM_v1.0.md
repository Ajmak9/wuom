# 2. The Three Structural Layers

## LAYER I — Grammar of Observation

**7 Principles**

---

## LAYER II — Regenerative Grammar

**4 Principles**

- Protects orientation.
- Prevents extractive dynamics.
- Positions AI, ecological time and shared grammar.

**Guiding question**

> How can extraction be avoided?

---

## LAYER III — Grammar of Recomposition

**Principle 12**

- Reconstructs the system after analysis.
- Relates the parts again.
- Restores the unity of the living system.

**Guiding question**

> How can the unity of the system be recovered?

# 7. Organizing Principle

BIOIA does not organize information; it organizes relationships.

BIOIA does not organize answers; it organizes questions.

BIOIA does not organize disciplines; it organizes orientation.

---

# 8. Final Synthesis

BIOIA / WUOM organizes a cognitive architecture for orienting the reading of living systems.

- The seven principles configure observation.
- The four principles protect regenerative orientation.
- Principle 12 recomposes relationships.
- Dynamic equilibrium emerges as a property of the system.
- Every new observation restarts the cycle.

---

# Closing

Every orientation begins with observation.

Every understanding requires relationships.

Every relationship requires recomposition.

Every equilibrium must continually renew itself.

BIOIA / WUOM does not seek to replace existing forms of knowledge.

Its purpose is to provide an architecture capable of reconnecting them in order to support more coherent orientation within living systems.

**Without stewardship there is no publication.**

**Without return there is no orientation.**
