## Editorial Principle

Every publication should open a way of observing, suggest a way of structuring, and activate a way of acting.

Without closing, imposing, or simplifying.

BIOIA/WUOM is an ecosystem that unfolds in layers.

Each layer does not replace the previous one; it expands it.

---

## Communication Method

The editorial principle is organized into three movements:

1. **Observe**  
   Open a way of looking at a signal, a territory, a relationship, or a problem.

2. **Structure**  
   Suggest a way of organizing what has been observed without reducing its complexity.

3. **Act**  
   Activate a practical, situated, and proportional orientation.

---

## Ethics of Discourse

BIOIA/WUOM communication avoids:

- closing the debate;
- imposing a conclusion;
- simplifying complex systems;
- turning communication into propaganda;
- replacing observation with slogans;
- presenting metaphors as absolute truths.

---

## Layer Logic

Publications do not function as isolated pieces.

Each publication adds a layer of reading to the ecosystem:

- an image may open an intuition;
- a question may orient reflection;
- a document may stabilize a structure;
- a publication may activate a conversation;
- a sequence may reveal a trajectory.

Each layer expands the system without replacing what came before.

---

## Application

This principle applies to:

- LinkedIn publications;
- public Notion pages;
- GitHub documents;
- visual images;
- reading metaphors;
- editorial sequences;
- communication of trees, principles, cases, and architectures.

---

## Operational Criterion

Before publishing, review:

1. Does the publication open a way of observing?
2. Does it suggest a reading structure?
3. Does it activate a possibility for action or orientation?
4. Does it avoid closing, imposing, or simplifying?
5. Does it expand a layer of the ecosystem without replacing previous ones?
