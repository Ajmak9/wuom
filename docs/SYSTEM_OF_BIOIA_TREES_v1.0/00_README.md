# 🌳 System of BIOIA Trees v1.0

**BIOIA / WUOM**

*A framework for reading living systems through complementary operational perspectives.*

---

## Overview

The **System of BIOIA Trees** organizes **seven complementary ways of reading** territories and living systems.

Each tree represents one operational dimension.

Together they provide an integrated framework for understanding complexity before acting.

BIOIA does not replace scientific methods, territorial knowledge or human experience.

It helps organize observation, interpretation, orientation and return.

---

# The Seven BIOIA Trees

## 🌳 01 · Horizon Tree

**Future · Scenarios · Orientation**

Opens the field of possible futures.

Questions:

- What futures are possible?
- Which opportunities are emerging?
- Which risks deserve attention?

---

## 🌳🌬️ 02 · Wind Tree

**Signals · Propagation · Movement**

Reads weak signals and how they spread.

Questions:

- What is beginning to move?
- Which signals are spreading?
- How do ideas and changes travel?

---

## 🌳☁️ 03 · Fog Tree

**Uncertainty · Ambiguity · Low Visibility**

Protects what is not yet sufficiently understood.

Questions:

- What do we still not know?
- What requires more observation?
- Which decisions should wait?

---

## 🌳🔥 04 · Fire Tree

**Transformation · Risk · Conscious Change**

Transforms with purpose and responsibility.

Questions:

- What needs to change?
- What should be preserved?
- How can transformation remain regenerative?

---

## 🌳 05 · Living Tree

**Maintenance · Care · Return**

Maintains life and closes the operational cycle.

Questions:

- What sustains the system?
- What requires care?
- What must return to the territory?

---

## 🌳⚡ 06 · Energy Tree

**Flow · Sources · Efficiency**

Reads the energetic metabolism of living systems.

Questions:

- Where does energy come from?
- How does it flow?
- Where is it being lost?

---

## 🌳⌛ 07 · Time Tree

**Rhythms · Temporal Scales · Maturity**

Organizes ecological and operational time.

Questions:

- When is the right moment?
- Which processes require patience?
- Which rhythms should not be accelerated?

---

# Purpose

The System of BIOIA Trees helps organize observation through complementary perspectives.

It supports:

- Territorial governance
- Ecological restoration
- Biodiversity
- Agriculture
- Climate adaptation
- Environmental planning
- Collective intelligence
- Responsible AI
- Decision support
- Living systems analysis

---

# Core Principles

- Stewardship before publication.
- Observation before intervention.
- Orientation before action.
- Return before extraction.
- Context before simplification.
- Living systems before isolated variables.
