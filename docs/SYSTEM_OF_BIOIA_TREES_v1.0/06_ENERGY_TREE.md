## 1. Definition

The Energy Tree is the tree that makes visible the flows that allow a system to function.

It does not study electricity alone.

It reads any form of energy capable of sustaining processes, transforming matter or maintaining life.

Every organization, ecosystem or territory depends on energetic flows.

Understanding them allows more efficient, resilient and sustainable decisions.

---

## 2. Function

The Energy Tree allows us to read:

- energy sources
- flows
- conversion
- distribution
- consumption
- losses
- efficiency
- regeneration capacity

Its main function is to understand how energy circulates within a living system.

---

## 3. Operational principle

> A system remains alive while it maintains a sufficient and balanced energetic flow.

The Energy Tree organizes this reading:

**source → capture → transformation → distribution → use → return → renewal**

---

## 4. Guiding questions

- Where does the energy come from?
- How does it circulate?
- Where do losses appear?
- What consumes the most energy?
- Which processes are most efficient?
- What dependencies exist?
- What regeneration capacity does the system have?

## 8. Ethics and custody

All energy implies responsibility.

Principles:

- do not waste resources
- do not consume more than can be regenerated
- prioritize efficiency before increasing consumption
- diversify sources
- avoid critical dependencies
- share energy when it strengthens the territory
- always verify ecological return

---

## 12. Closing

The Energy Tree reminds us that no system can be sustained if it does not understand how it obtains, transforms and returns the energy it uses.

Efficiency is not only about consuming less.

It is about maintaining the right flow so life can continue.

Every energy input must have a purpose.

Every energy use must generate return.

**Without custody, there is no publication.**  
**Without return, there is no orientation.**
