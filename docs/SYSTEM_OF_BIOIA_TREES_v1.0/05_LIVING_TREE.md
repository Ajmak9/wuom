# 05 · Living Tree
## Maintenance · Return · Cycle · Sustaining

### Status
BIOIA / WUOM base document  
Operational tree of life, balance and return  
Version: v0.1

---

## 1. Definition

The Living Tree is the tree that sustains the cycle.

It does not open futures by itself.  
It does not disperse signals.  
It does not contain fog.  
It does not transform through fire.

It integrates the previous dimensions and verifies that the system remains alive.

It is the tree of maintenance, care, balance and return to origin.

---

## 2. Function

The Living Tree allows us to read:

- life cycles
- balance
- continuity
- maintenance
- care
- return
- regeneration
- real sustainability

Its main function is to ensure that every orientation returns to the territory without breaking the cycle.

---

## 3. Operational principle

> Transforming is not enough.  
> What has been transformed must be sustained.

The Living Tree organizes this reading:

**action → care → integration → balance → return → living cycle**

---

## 4. Guiding questions

- What needs care?
- What still sustains the life of the system?
- What balance must be maintained?
- What must return to the territory?
- What is being exhausted?
- What needs regeneration?
- What allows the cycle to continue?

---

## 5. Signals observed

The Living Tree observes:

- natural rhythms
- recovery capacity
- system fatigue
- loss of balance
- available resources
- relationships of care
- regeneration signals
- continuity of cycles
- return of benefits
- concrete sustainability

---

## 6. The living as closure of the cycle

The Living Tree is not passive.

Maintaining is also acting.

Caring is also deciding.

Returning is also producing orientation.

In BIOIA, no process is complete unless it returns to the territory in a way that is understandable, useful and verifiable.

---

## 7. BIOIA applications

The Living Tree can be applied to:

- territorial regeneration
- agriculture and living soil
- biodiversity
- governance of return
- care of complex systems
- impact assessment
- project continuity
- community maintenance
- ecological restoration
- learning cycles

---

## 8. Ethics and custody

The Living Tree prevents orientation from becoming extraction.

Principles:

- do not take without returning
- do not intervene without sustaining
- do not measure without caring
- do not transform without maintaining
- do not publish without return
- do not break living cycles for apparent efficiency
- always verify the real effect on the territory

---

## 9. Relation with other trees

### With the Horizon Tree

The Horizon opens futures.  
The Living Tree asks whether those futures are habitable.

### With the Wind Tree

The Wind disperses signals.  
The Living Tree verifies which ones nourish the system.

### With the Fog Tree

The Fog contains uncertainty.  
The Living Tree protects the time needed for life to mature.

### With the Fire Tree

The Fire transforms.  
The Living Tree ensures transformation does not destroy the cycle.

---

## 10. Expected result

The Living Tree produces:

- system continuity
- real regeneration
- territorial return
- operational balance
- sustained care
- verifiable cycles
- integrated orientation

---

## 11. Short formula

> Living systems do not only change.  
> They return, care and sustain.

---

## 12. Closing

The Living Tree closes the BIOIA Tree System.

Without it, the Horizon may become fantasy.

The Wind may become dispersion.

The Fog may become blockage.

The Fire may become destruction.

The Living Tree restores balance.

It ensures that every change, every signal, every pause and every projection returns to the cycle of life.

**Without custody, there is no publication.**  
**Without return, there is no orientation.**
